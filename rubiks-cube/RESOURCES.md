# Rubik's Cube Resources

## Knowledge

- [You CAN Do the Rubiks Cube — Official Beginner Solution Guide (PDF)](https://www.youcandothecube.com/solve-it/3-x-3-solution)
  The canonical beginner layer-by-layer method. Use for: cross, F2L (beginner style), OLL, PLL step reference.

- [J Perm — Beginner's Method Tutorial (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Ron6MN45LY)
  High-quality video walkthrough of the full beginner method. Use for: visual algorithm demonstrations.

- [Ruwix — Interactive 3×3 Simulator](https://ruwix.com/the-rubiks-cube/notation/)
  Browser-based cube simulator + notation reference. Use for: practicing moves without a physical cube.

- [Rubik's Cube Notation — Ruwix](https://ruwix.com/the-rubiks-cube/notation/)
  Complete move notation reference (U, D, L, R, F, B, M, E, S and their primes). Use for: reading algorithms.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/Cubers](https://reddit.com/r/Cubers)
  Active, beginner-friendly community. Use for: solve troubleshooting, progress sharing, next-step advice.

- [SpeedSolving.com Forums](https://www.speedsolving.com/forum/)
  Deeper technical community. Use for: algorithm variants, method comparisons after first solve achieved.
