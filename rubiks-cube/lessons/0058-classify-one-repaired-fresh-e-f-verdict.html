Rubik’s Cube · F2L evidence practice

Classify one repaired Fresh E/F verdict

After a minimal repair, make one decision: is the sentence ready to guide practice, or does it still need matching evidence?

⏱ 5–7 minutes · One skill: choose ACT or FRESH EVIDENCE

The classification gate

1 · ConditionDo Fresh E and F match on pair, workspace, retained cue, and checkpoint?
2 · EvidenceDoes the sentence report the recorded first mismatch accurately?
3 · ActionDoes it state exactly one action that follows from that evidence?

All three pass → ACT. A condition failure → FRESH EVIDENCE, even if the written repair is otherwise sensible.

Worked classification

Fresh E and F, matching condition
blue-red pair · empty front-right workspace · say “edge-centre” before setup · pre-trigger checkpoint

Observed first mismatch, both attempts
edge-centre check fails at checkpoint
Repaired verdict: Because Fresh E and F repeat an edge-centre failure, slow the recognition drill.

Condition matches; the evidence says exactly what both logs show; and there is one matched action. Label it ACT.

Different condition
Fresh E: cue before setup · Fresh F: cue after setup
Repaired verdict: Because Fresh F’s cue began after setup, collect a matching replacement attempt.

This is correctly repaired, but it cannot authorize a practice change yet. Label it FRESH EVIDENCE.

Quiz 1: decide the label

Fresh E and F share all four condition fields. Both first fail the green-light check. The repaired sentence is: “Because both first fail the green light, slow the green-light recognition drill.”

Quiz 2: do not confuse a repair with a verdict

The evidence clause was repaired accurately, but Fresh F used an empty front-left workspace while Fresh E used front-right. The sentence now says: “Collect a matching Fresh F attempt with an empty front-right workspace.”

One cube-side action

  1. Take one repaired verdict from yesterday’s audit.
  2. Run the gate in order: condition → evidence → one action.
  3. Write ACT only if every check passes; otherwise write FRESH EVIDENCE and name the matching replacement attempt.

Ask your teacher to check any verdict that seems to have two “reasonable” actions. A controlled drill gets one next action.
