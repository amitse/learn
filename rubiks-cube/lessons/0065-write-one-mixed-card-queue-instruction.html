Rubik's Cube · Lesson 65 · under 10 minutes

Write one queue instruction from a mixed card

Your goal is a repeatable solve. Today, turn a reviewed F2L triage card into one safe next action—without blending evidence between rows.

The one rule

Choose one row, then write only the action that its classification permits. An ACT row supplies one evidence-matched practice instruction. A FRESH EVIDENCE row supplies one matching replacement-attempt instruction. It does not supply a drill change.

Use this tiny writing loop

1
Read the row label.
ACT = perform the row’s one approved action. FRESH EVIDENCE = collect its replacement attempt under the stated matching conditions.
2
Copy the row’s scope.
Keep its target pair, empty-workspace type, retained cue, and checkpoint together. Those are the comparison conditions—not optional decoration.
3
Write one verb-led line.
Start with Run, Collect, or Repeat. Stop after one action. Do not append “then also…”

Worked card → queue

Row A · ACT
Fresh E and F matched. Both first failed at the green-light check. Approved action: retain the pair/workspace/cue and run five slow green-light reads before any trigger.
Row B · FRESH EVIDENCE
Fresh F used a front-left workspace while Fresh E used front-right. Replacement requirement: target blue-red pair, front-right empty workspace, say “white right; edge matches front centre” before the trigger, checkpoint immediately before the trigger.

Choose Row B. The correct queue line is:

Collect one replacement Fresh F attempt for the blue-red pair with a front-right empty workspace; say “white right; edge matches front centre” before the trigger and record the checkpoint immediately before the trigger.

Notice what is absent: no five-repetition drill, no explanation for the drift, and no action imported from Row A.

Quick check

Row C · ACT: Fresh E and F are comparable and both repeat the same planning mismatch. The approved action says: “slow the named U-setup cue for five repetitions under the same pair and workspace.” Which queue instruction is valid?

Do it now

Take your next audited mixed card and select one row. Write its queue line in this exact shape:

[Verb] [one action] for [row’s condition], checked at [row’s checkpoint].

If you cannot finish that sentence without adding a second verb, split it: pick only the first permitted action. Ask your teacher to review the sentence before you practise.
