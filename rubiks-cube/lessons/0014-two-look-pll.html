2-Look PLL

Permute the last layer in at most 2 algorithm executions — completing your full solve.

✓ White Cross ✓ White Corners ✓ F2L ✓ 2-Look OLL → 2-Look PLL
🎯 What you'll learn: How to permute (move into correct position) the last layer's pieces using just 3 algorithms in at most 2 passes — giving you a complete, repeatable solve from any scramble.

What is PLL?

PLL = Permutation of the Last Layer. After OLL, every top sticker is yellow — but the edge and corner pieces may not be in the right spots. PLL fixes that without disturbing the orientation.

Full PLL has 21 algorithms. 2-Look PLL handles every case with just 3 algorithms and at most 2 passes:

Look 1 — Corner Permutation

Look at the 4 top-layer corners. Ask: are any 2 corners in their correct position (colours match their adjacent center)?

Recognition rule: Find a side of the cube where both top corners have the same colour on that face. Those 2 corners are "solved" (relative to each other). Keep that face at the back, then run the Y-Perm.
Y-Perm (corner 3-cycle)
F R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R F'
Cycles 3 corners: front-right ↔ front-left ↔ back-right. The back-left corner stays put. After this, all 4 corners are in their correct positions.

Recognition cases

What you seeAction
All 4 corners already correctSkip to Look 2
Exactly 2 adjacent corners match on one sidePlace that side at back → Y-Perm
No matching pair at all (diagonal swap)Apply Y-Perm from any angle → creates a matching pair → apply again
⚠️ Common mistake: Turning the U face to "find" a matching pair will break the OLL you just solved. Always use whole-cube rotations (y / y') to look around.

Look 2 — Edge Permutation

Once corners are solved, check the 4 top-layer edges. One of three things is true:

  1. All 4 edges correct → Solved! 🎉
  2. 3 edges need to cycle clockwise → Ua-Perm
  3. 3 edges need to cycle counter-clockwise → Ub-Perm

One edge will always be in the correct position already — that's your anchor. Put that edge at the back.

Ua-Perm (edges cycle counter-clockwise: L→F→R)
R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2
The front, left, and right edges cycle. Back edge stays put. Mnemonic: "U-inverse, U, U, U, U-inverse, U-inverse" wraps around the right side.
Ub-Perm (edges cycle clockwise: R→F→L)
R2 U R U R' U' R' U' R' U R'
Mirror of Ua-Perm. Cycles the same 3 edges in the opposite direction. Easiest approach: just perform Ua-Perm twice if you struggle to distinguish.

How to tell Ua vs Ub

With the solved edge at the back, look at the front edge's sticker colour:

  • If the front sticker should go left → Ua-Perm
  • If the front sticker should go right → Ub-Perm

Or simply: apply Ua-Perm. If edges are solved → done. If not, apply Ua-Perm again (= Ub-Perm effect).

The Complete Solve Checklist

  1. White cross on bottom
  2. White corners (complete white face)
  3. Second layer edges (F2L beginner)
  4. Yellow cross on top (OLL Look 1)
  5. Orient yellow corners (OLL Look 2 — Sune/Anti-Sune)
  6. Permute corners (PLL Look 1 — Y-Perm if needed)
  7. Permute edges (PLL Look 2 — Ua/Ub-Perm if needed)
  8. ✅ Solved!

Practice Tips

Drill order: Learn Ua-Perm first (11 moves, very rhythmic). Then learn Ub-Perm. Practice Y-Perm last — it's the longest but only happens in corners step.
Virtual cube: Use alg.cubing.net to animate any algorithm and see it step-by-step before trying on a physical cube.
