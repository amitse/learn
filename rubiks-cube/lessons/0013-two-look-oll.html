2-Look OLL: Orient the Last Layer in Two Steps

Replace the beginner's last-layer routine with two focused looks — and just two algorithms.

⏱ ~8 min 🔢 2 new algorithms 🎯 CFOP entry

Where You Are in the Solve

You've completed F2L — the first two layers are solved. Now you need to handle the top (last) layer. In the beginner method this felt like 3 or 4 separate steps. OLL collapses it into one goal: make the entire top face yellow.

✓ White Cross ✓ White Corners ✓ F2L (2nd Layer) → OLL (orient top) PLL (permute top)

OLL stands for Orientation of the Last Layer. After OLL, every sticker on the top face is yellow. After the following step (PLL), the cube is solved.

Full OLL = 57 algorithms. Nobody starts there. 2-Look OLL gets the same result using just 2 algorithms and at most 2 looks.

Look 1 Is Already Done — The Yellow Cross

You learned this in Lesson 5. The yellow cross algorithm (F (R U R' U') F') makes a yellow cross on the top face.

✅ After Look 1 you have: yellow cross on top, four yellow edge stickers, yellow center. The four corner stickers on top may still be non-yellow.

That's exactly what Look 2 fixes.

The Two Algorithms

Sune

Use when the one correct corner is at back-left (UBL).

Sune
R  U  R'  U  R  U2  R'
MoveWhat it does
RRight face clockwise — lifts FR corner to top
UTop face clockwise — positions the lifted corner
R'Right face counter-clockwise — returns right face
UTop face clockwise again
RRight face clockwise
U2Top face double — the twist that orients
R'Right face counter-clockwise — restore

Anti-Sune

Use when the one correct corner is at back-right (UBR).

Anti-Sune
R'  U'  R  U'  R'  U2  R

Anti-Sune is the exact mirror of Sune — every move flipped. If Sune feels natural, Anti-Sune should too after a few repetitions.

How to Solve Every Case Step-by-Step

Case: 4 yellow corners

OLL is done. Proceed to PLL.

Case: 1 yellow corner

  1. Identify the one corner that has yellow facing up on the top face.
  2. Rotate the whole cube (not the U layer) with y or y' until that corner sits at back-left → do Sune.
  3. If yellow is at back-right instead, rotate until it's back-right → do Anti-Sune.

Case: 0 yellow corners

  1. Hold the cube in any orientation (cross still on top).
  2. Execute Sune once. Now check the corners again.
  3. You will now have exactly 1 yellow corner. Follow the 1-corner procedure above.
Maximum 2 Sune/Anti-Sune executions to orient any top face. That's why it's called 2-Look OLL.

Practice Drill

  • Solve to the end of F2L on a real or virtual cube (Ruwix simulator works).
  • Do the yellow cross algorithm if needed: F (R U R' U') F'
  • Count yellow corners on top. Identify your case.
  • Apply Sune or Anti-Sune (with correct setup). Check: now all yellow?
  • Repeat 5 complete solves. Note: did you ever need more than 2 algorithm executions for OLL?
Benchmark: After 10 solves, you should be selecting the right algorithm without needing to think about it. Hesitation on the "0-corner" case is normal at first.
