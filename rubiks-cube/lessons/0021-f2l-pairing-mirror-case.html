Pair the left-side mirror

Lesson 20 gave you one right-side F2L picture. This is its mirror: move the empty working slot to front-left, make the mirrored picture, then use the mirrored trigger.

The one mirrored picture

Hold white down, yellow up, with an empty front-left target slot. Put the target corner at UFL (upper-front-left) with its white sticker facing left. Put its matching edge at UF, with its front-facing colour matching the front centre.

Same identity rule; opposite workspace. Name the corner’s two non-white colours, find that exact edge, and verify the slot from the centres. Only then use this picture.

Turn the mirror picture into a pair

When that picture is ready above an empty front-left slot, use the left trigger:

L′ U′ L

L′ opens the front-left working space.
U′ brings the edge into the corner’s path.
L closes the slot with the two pieces together.

The sequence is not a new idea: it is the left-hand mirror of Lesson 20’s R U R′. Make the picture first; then execute the three moves as one smooth chunk.

Choose the safe side

You now have two small options, not a 41-case catalogue. If a recognized pair can become the right-facing picture above an empty front-right slot, use R U R′. If it can become today’s left-facing picture above an empty front-left slot, use L′ U′ L.

Do not force a trigger through a solved pair. A whole-cube y rotation can bring the relevant empty slot to the front; U turns can prepare top-layer pieces. If neither safe picture appears, choose another pair or reset by ejecting safely.

One deliberate rep

Try this on a real or virtual cube: after your cross, find an unsolved pair using the colour chain. Put its empty slot front-left. Use only U moves until the corner is at UFL with white facing left and the matching edge is at UF with its front colour matching the front centre. Say “left picture ready,” do L′ U′ L, then inspect the slot.

A mirror case doubles your choices while keeping the mental load small. Ask your teacher a follow-up question if the pair fails to join; include the slot, white-sticker direction, and edge position you saw before turning.
