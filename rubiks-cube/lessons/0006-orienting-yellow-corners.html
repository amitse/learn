Layers 1 and 2 are solved. The top face has a yellow cross (4 edges + center). The 4 corner cells may show yellow — or may not. This lesson makes all of them yellow.

Hold the cube yellow on top, white on bottom for this entire step. Don't flip it.

What you're looking at

Look down at the top face. The cross cells (edges + center) are already yellow. The 4 corners are what vary. A dark corner = a non-yellow sticker is facing up.

Top face after Yellow Cross — example starting state
BLBBR LCR FLFFR

BL/BR/FL/FR = corners. B/F/L/R = edges (always yellow after lesson 5). C = center (always yellow).

The algorithm: Sune

Sune (ROO-neh) is the single algorithm for this step. It's 7 moves. It leaves the back-right (BR) corner completely untouched while cycling and reorienting the other three.

Source: Ruwix — Beginner's Method · J Perm — OLL Algorithms

Memory hook: The first 4 moves are almost a double Right Trigger (R U R′ U) — but then instead of ending, you continue: R U2 R′. The U2 (double turn) before the last R′ is the distinguishing feature. Say it aloud: "R U R-prime U, R U-two R-prime."
Don't confuse with lesson 5: The Yellow Cross algorithm was F R U R′ U′ F′. Sune is different — no F moves, starts and ends on R.

The 3-step rule

Repeat this loop until all 4 corners show yellow on top:

  1. Scan: Look at the 4 corners of the top face. Find one showing yellow on top.
  2. Position: Use U moves (top-layer rotations only — don't rotate the whole cube) to slide that corner to the back-right (BR) position. The cross stays intact because U moves only shift top-layer pieces.
  3. Apply Sune: Execute R U R′ U R U2 R′. The BR corner stays yellow-up. Others may change. Check and repeat.
⚠ What if 0 corners show yellow? Apply Sune once from any hold. At least one corner will become yellow-up. Then use the 3-step loop above.
Why U moves and not y-rotations? A U move only rotates the top layer — it moves pieces around the top face without changing which sticker faces up. So you can reposition corners freely using U without losing any progress on the cross or other corners.

✅ What you learned this lesson
  • Sune: R U R′ U R U2 R′ — the only algorithm for this step
  • BR corner must show yellow on top before applying Sune
  • Use U moves only (not whole-cube rotations) to position yellow at BR
  • Apply 1–3 Sunes depending on your starting case; worst case is always solved
  • If 0 corners yellow: apply Sune blindly once, then use the 3-step loop
