Use a U turn to make the pair picture

Your corner and matching edge can both be on top yet still be separated. Don’t insert blindly: use a U setup to make one of the two safe pictures you already know, then trigger.

The single idea: setup, then trigger

Hold white down. Pick an empty F2L working slot and bring it to the front with a whole-cube y rotation. If the target corner and its matching edge are both on U but not in a safe pairing picture, turn U or U′ until they are.

Setup turns are allowed because they preserve completed bottom slots. A U turn moves only top-layer pieces. A trigger into a solved/protected slot is the dangerous move.

Your two destination pictures

Right picture: empty front-right slot; corner at UFR with white facing right; matching edge at UF, front sticker matches front centre. Then R U R′.
Left picture: empty front-left slot; corner at UFL with white facing left; matching edge at UF, front sticker matches front centre. Then L′ U′ L.

You are not learning a third insertion. You are learning to choose a U-layer setup that lands in a known picture.

A compact decision loop

1. Name it. Read the corner’s two non-white colours → find the exact edge → identify the centre-defined slot.

2. Protect it. Rotate an empty target slot to front; never use a solved slot as your workspace.

3. Set it up. Use U or U′ to seek either destination picture above.

4. Commit once. Only when the picture’s white direction and edge/centre check agree, do its trigger.

If one U turn does not make a safe picture, inspect again rather than forcing it. This lesson’s win is deliberate setup, not finishing every F2L case quickly.

One deliberate rep

On a real or virtual cube, solve the cross and find one unsolved F2L pair with both pieces on U. Bring its empty slot to the front. Before turning, say: “I will use U to make the right picture” (or left). Make the picture, run the matching trigger, then inspect that the pair is inserted and other solved slots survived.

Ask your teacher a follow-up question if a setup is confusing. Include the corner position, white-sticker direction, edge position, and which slot was empty before your U turn.
