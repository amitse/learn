Rubik's Cube · Lesson 78 · under 10 minutes

Repair one minimally classified queue decision

Turn a MINIMAL REPAIR NEEDED decision into a usable instruction without smuggling in new cube plans.

Today’s one skill

Repair only the earliest failed clause, then run the complete audit again. A repair is not permission to improve every sentence, diagnose the cube, or add an insertion.

The repair-and-re-audit loop

1 · Locate the first failure. Audit in order: literal evidence → copied ACT-row permission → four scope fields → exactly one action.
2 · Replace only that clause. Copy the matching fact from the checkpoint log or source ACT row. Preserve all clauses that already pass.
3 · Re-audit from the start. The repaired decision is ACTION-READY only if every gate now passes. Otherwise repair the newly first failure—not a later one.

Worked repair: scope drift

Valid checkpoint log
Rep 4 — immediately before trigger: corner white faces Right; matching edge’s front sticker matches the front centre.
Selected ACT row
Run five slow green-light reads.
Classified decision — MINIMAL REPAIR NEEDED
“Because Rep 4 showed white facing Right and the edge matching the front centre immediately before trigger, run five slow green-light reads for the blue-red pair in a front-left empty slot, saying ‘white right; edge matches front centre’ before trigger.”

The evidence and permission pass. The first failure is the workspace: the source scope says front-right, not front-left.

Minimal repair
Replace only front-left with front-right. Do not alter the evidence, cue timing, or approved five-read action.
Re-audit result — ACTION-READY
The repaired sentence now has literal evidence, copied permission, all four scope fields, and one action. It may guide exactly the five-read drill and then stops.

Do not “help” the repair

These edits cross the repair boundary even though they may sound sensible:

Adding “then insert with R U R′” creates a second action.
Changing the observation to “the pair was ready” replaces literal evidence with a conclusion.
Switching to a different pair changes scope rather than repairing the decision.

Quick check

An audit finds the first failure at the action gate: the decision correctly states the evidence and all four scope fields, but says “run five slow green-light reads, then insert.” What is the minimal repair?

Do it now

Take one decision labelled MINIMAL REPAIR NEEDED. Point to its first failed clause, replace only that text from its source, then say the four audit gates aloud. Ask your teacher to check a borderline repair before acting on it.
