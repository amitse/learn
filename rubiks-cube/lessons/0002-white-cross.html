The White Cross

Layer-by-layer · Step 1 of 7 · ~10 min

🎯

Your win today: Starting from a scrambled cube, build the white cross — 4 white edge pieces in a cross on the white face, each matching the adjacent center color.

What is the White Cross?

The white cross is the very first step. It's not just a cross shape on the white face — it also needs the correct alignment: each white edge piece's second colour must match the centre of that side.

The centre pieces never move. The green centre is always green, the red centre always red. Those centres are your compass for the whole solve. ↗ ruwix.com

The Daisy Method

The easiest way to build the white cross is in two stages via a "daisy". Here's the full flow:

1 Orient: Yellow on top

Hold the cube so the yellow centre faces up (U face). White centre will be on the bottom (D face). This is your starting position for the whole solve.

2 Build the Daisy

Find all 4 white edge pieces anywhere on the cube. Move them one by one to the top (U) face, surrounding the yellow centre — white sticker facing up. The result looks like a daisy: yellow in the middle, 4 white petals.

3 Match & Lock each petal

For each white edge (daisy petal): rotate the U face until the petal's side colour aligns above the matching centre. Then do F2 to drive it to the bottom cross. Repeat for all 4 petals.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Stage 1 — Building the Daisy

A white edge piece has two stickers: white and one other colour. It can be in 4 places on the cube — and each needs a different technique to get it to the daisy position.

Case A — white edge is already on the top layer (U face), white sticker facing sideways:
Rotate U until the piece is above its matching centre, then do F U R' or just check if U turns alone can get white sticker facing up. Usually one or two moves.

Case B — white edge is in the middle layer (E layer):
Pick the front face of that piece, do F to bring it to the top. Adjust with U turns.

Case C — white edge is in the bottom layer (D face), white sticker facing down:

F2
Rotate the front face twice — the piece hops to the top layer, white sticker now facing up. Then move the daisy to accommodate it with U turns.

Case D — white edge is in the bottom layer, white sticker facing sideways:

F U R U'
Front, U, Right, U-prime — lifts the edge into the daisy position.
💡 Tip: You don't need to memorise exact algorithms for the daisy — just fiddle. The goal is simple: get 4 white-sticker-up pieces around the yellow centre. If you accidentally knock one out, it's fine, fix it. This stage is meant to be exploratory.

Stage 2 — Matching and Locking Petals

Once you have the daisy, this stage is purely mechanical:

1 Look at a petal's side colour

Each daisy petal (white edge on U) shows a second colour on the side. For example: the white-green edge has a green sticker facing outward on the U layer.

2 Rotate U until it aligns

Turn the U face until that green sticker is directly above the green centre. This "aims" the petal at its home.

3 F2 — done

Double-turn the front face (F2). The white-green edge drops to the bottom, white sticker on the D face, green sticker matching the green centre. ✅

4 Repeat for remaining 3 petals

Each F2 preserves the petals already locked in. Work around the cube until all 4 are in the white cross.

Check Your White Cross

Flip the cube so white is on top. Verify all 4 conditions:

  • White sticker in the top position of each cross arm ✓
  • White centre in the middle ✓
  • Front arm's side sticker = front centre colour ✓
  • Right, Back, Left arms similarly match ✓
