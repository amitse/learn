Rubik’s Cube · under 10 minutes

Write a Fresh E/F verdict

Convert two comparable F2L observations into one specific next action—without adding a story the evidence does not support.

The one thing to learn

Once Fresh E and Fresh F are comparable, write one verdict sentence. It must name the evidence and exactly one justified action. This keeps a practice log useful when you return to it tomorrow.

Verdict template: “Because Fresh E and F [same evidence], [one action].” No new diagnosis belongs in that sentence.

Four evidence → action pairs

Both clear target
Retain
“E and F both moved past planning; retain the named-setup cue.”
Either repeats target
Refine or slow
“F repeated planning; slow or minimally refine the planning drill.”
Both share later mismatch
Successor
“E and F share execution after a green light; retain the cue and target execution next.”
Conditions differ
Repair evidence
“E and F used different workspace types; collect matching fresh attempts.”

A shared later mismatch supports a successor only after both logs have cleared the old target. “Later” follows the workflow: planning → green-light recognition → authorized-trigger execution.

Worked example

Tested target: green-light recognition. Fixed condition: same pair, empty workspace, retained cue, and checkpoint.

Because Fresh E and F both cleared green-light recognition and shared an execution mismatch, retain the green-light cue and target execution next.

Notice what it does not claim: that execution is “bad,” why it happened, or which extra move to use. Those would need new evidence.

Write it in 45 seconds

  1. Write the tested successor target.
  2. Confirm E and F match the written condition.
  3. Name each first mismatch.
  4. Choose one evidence → action pair above.
  5. Complete the template in one sentence. Stop there.

Interactive verdict

Target: planning. Both fresh attempts have the same pair, workspace, retained cue, and checkpoint. Fresh E’s first mismatch is green-light recognition; Fresh F’s first mismatch is also green-light recognition. Which is the evidence-backed sentence?

Choose one answer for instant feedback.

Today’s tangible win

After your next Fresh E/F pair, write one sentence using the template. Share that sentence and the two first-mismatch labels with your teacher if you want help checking the classification.
