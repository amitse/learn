Check the edge before the trigger

Your corner can look ready and still make a bad F2L insertion. Today’s win: use one fast edge-to-centre check to decide whether the familiar right or left picture is actually safe.

One idea: the edge has a direction too

In the two safe pictures you already know, the matching edge sits at UF. Before any trigger, inspect the sticker on the front of that edge. It must match the front centre.

Green front centre + green front edge stickerEdge agrees with the front. If the corner also has the correct white direction, this is a confirmed safe picture.
Green front centre + red front edge stickerEdge does not agree with the front. The pieces may be the right pair, but this is not the known picture. Do not fire the trigger.
Important distinction: pair identity comes from the corner’s two non-white colours. The centre check tests the current orientation and relationship of that same matching edge.

The two-part green light

With white down and an empty working slot at the front, give yourself permission to insert only when both checks pass:

Right pictureCorner at UFR, white facing Right; matching edge at UF; edge’s front sticker = front centre. Then R U R′.
Left pictureCorner at UFL, white facing Left; matching edge at UF; edge’s front sticker = front centre. Then L′ U′ L.

A correct-looking corner is only half the picture. Centres are fixed, so they are the reliable referee for the edge.

When the check fails: set up, then re-read

Stop before the trigger. Name the pair by the corner’s two non-white colours. Confirm that the working slot is empty; solved pairs stay protected.
Try a top-layer setup only. Use U, U′, or U2 to change the corner–edge relationship. This is exploration toward a known picture, not a command to insert.
Rebuild both checks. Place the corner in the appropriate UFR/UFL position with white facing the side, bring its matching edge to UF, then compare the edge’s front sticker with the front centre.
Insert only on green. If corner direction and edge-centre match agree, use the matching short trigger. If not, stop and choose a different pair or reset the empty working slot with a whole-cube rotation.
Safety boundary: a U setup preserves solved F2L pairs below. A trigger through a solved slot does not. Keep the workspace empty.

One deliberate rep

Pick a recognized F2L pair on U. Rotate an empty target slot to the front. Build either the right or left picture, then say out loud: “white direction; edge colour; centre colour.” Insert only if the last two colours match. If they do not, make one U-layer setup and re-read before doing anything else.

Ask your teacher a follow-up if a setup feels impossible. Report the corner’s three colours, the matching edge’s two colours, which sticker faced front at UF, the front-centre colour, and which empty slot you protected.
