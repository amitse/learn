Rubik’s Cube · Lesson 31 ~7 minutes

Diagnose a failed F2L green light

You can now name the U setup. The next skill is stopping correctly when that setup reaches the right positions but the pair is still not safe to insert—so you protect your solved slots instead of forcing a trigger.

The one distinction

Positional successThe corner and edge reach the destination locations: right case UFR + UF, or left case UFL + UF.
Insertion authorizationThe full green light also needs the white-sticker direction and the edge-centre match. Positions alone do not authorize a trigger.

If either check fails: do not use the insertion trigger.

Keep the empty working slot safe. Re-read the pair; if the corner is white-up or the edge remains unready, use the existing one-trigger normalization rule through an empty slot, then stop and read again. If the pair is awkward, choosing another unsolved pair is also correct.

Two quick diagnoses

A — Right-side destination, wrong edge.
You named U′. Now the corner is at UFR with white facing Right, and the matching edge is at UF—but its front sticker does not match the front centre.
Diagnosis: positional success; edge-readiness failure. Stop. Do not do R U R′.
B — Left-side destination, wrong corner orientation.
The edge at UF passes its front-centre check, but the corner at UFL has white facing Up.
Diagnosis: positional success; corner-orientation failure. Stop. A U turn cannot change white-up into white-left.

The safe response is not “try the trigger and see.” It is read → normalize through an empty slot if needed → re-read.

60-second check

1. Your named setup puts the pair at UFR + UF, but the edge’s front sticker mismatches the front centre. What next?

2. Why is a white-up corner not fixed by trying all four U setups?

On-cube win

  1. Choose one unsolved pair and an empty working slot.
  2. Use your Lesson 30 destination map to name exactly one setup.
  3. Make it once. Say: “positions: yes/no; corner: yes/no; edge: yes/no.”
  4. Only insert on yes / yes / yes. Otherwise, stop and take the safe recovery route.

This is how you turn F2L from hopeful turning into reliable diagnosis—the mission-critical habit for completing solves without restarting.
