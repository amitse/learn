Rubik's Cube · Lesson 4 of 8 · ~8 min

Second Layer Edges

Complete Layer 2 — the middle band of the cube.

Where you are

Layer 1 is finished — white face solved, four white corners in place. Now flip the cube over so white is on the bottom, yellow on top. You'll hold it this way for the rest of the solve.

Cube orientation from now on
  • White face DOWN (D). Yellow face UP (U). Keep this throughout Layers 2 and 3.
  • The 4 middle-layer slots sit between the four side centres. Your goal: fill all 4 with the right edge pieces.
  • A middle-layer edge has no yellow sticker. If an edge piece has a yellow sticker, it belongs in the last layer — skip it for now.

Finding your target edges

Scan the top layer (U face) for edge pieces with no yellow sticker. Each one belongs in the middle band.

💡

If a non-yellow edge is already in the middle layer but in the wrong slot or orientation, you'll handle it last — see Extraction below.

Setup: bring the edge above its slot

For each target edge:

  1. Identify which two centre colours the edge should sit between (the edge's two sticker colours match two adjacent centres).
  2. Rotate U until the edge is directly above the matching column — the side sticker of the edge faces the same direction as its matching centre.
  3. Turn the whole cube (y rotation) so the target slot is at front-right. Now look at how the edge is oriented on the U face.

Easy way to remember which case: look at the side sticker of the edge (the one not on the U face). If it faces the front → Right Insert. If it faces the right → Left Insert.

Extraction: edge stuck in the middle layer

Sometimes you'll find a correct-looking edge already in the middle layer — but flipped the wrong way, or in the wrong slot. You can't insert over it; you need to pop it out first.

⚠️

Do this: Align the cube so the stuck edge is at the front-right slot. Apply either insertion algorithm (Right or Left) from the top — this bumps the stuck piece up to the U layer and inserts whatever was on top. Then reinsert the freed piece normally.

  1. Rotate the cube so the mis-inserted edge is at front-right.
  2. Apply any second-layer insertion algorithm (e.g. Right Insert) — this pops the edge to the top layer.
  3. Now treat the freed edge as a normal top-layer edge and reinsert it correctly.

Putting it together

Repeat for all four middle-layer edges. The order doesn't matter.

Full Layer 2 checklist
  1. White face down, yellow up.
  2. Scan the U layer for edges with no yellow sticker.
  3. For each: rotate U to set up, orient cube so target slot is front-right.
  4. Identify Case A or B by looking at the side sticker.
  5. Execute the algorithm. Edge inserts cleanly.
  6. Extract any mis-inserted edges and redo them.
  7. Repeat until all 4 middle slots are filled.
💡

Algorithms look long at first. After 5–6 repetitions, your hands start to do them automatically. Focus on understanding the setup first — the algorithm execution will follow with practice. Use Ruwix online solver or alg.cubing.net to visualise the algorithms in 3D.
