Choose the least-disruptive recovery

A failed green light is not an invitation to turn more. Your win today: name the failure, then make the smallest safe next move while preserving solved pairs.

The recovery rule

Do not insert on a failed green light. First decide what failed. Use a U setup only for a positional mismatch; use one normalization trigger through an empty slot for an orientation/readiness failure; switch pairs if another pair is ready.

Three diagnoses → three responses

Position failure
The target pieces are not at the selected destination positions (UFR + UF right, or UFL + UF left). Response: use the one named 0 / U / U2 / U′ setup only if the position template says it is viable. Otherwise re-read; do not hunt with U turns.
Corner-orientation failure
The pair reached its destination positions, but the corner’s white sticker faces Up rather than the selected side. Response: a U move cannot change this direction. Protect an empty working slot, normalize once with its short trigger, then re-read.
Edge-readiness failure
The matching edge is at UF, but its front sticker does not match the front centre. Response: do not insert. Normalize once through the empty slot, then re-identify the pair and run the full check again.

Priority when more than one action is possible

  1. Insert only a fully green-lit pair. That is the least disruptive successful action.
  2. If none is ready, take another ready unsolved pair rather than disturbing this one.
  3. If this is the only useful pair, one controlled normalization through an empty working slot is next.
  4. After every recovery move, stop and re-read. Never chain recovery triggers on a guess.

Whole-cube y rotations choose your viewpoint; they do not repair an F2L relationship. A U turn changes location, not a white-up corner’s orientation.

Quick decision drill

Choose the least-disruptive next action in each case. Feedback appears immediately.

1. Right-side destination positions are correct. The corner’s white sticker faces Up. The edge passes its centre check; another unsolved pair is fully green-lit elsewhere.

2. The selected pair is at UFR + UF. White faces Right, but the edge’s front sticker disagrees with the front centre. No other pair is ready, and front-right is empty.

3. The corner and edge are not in the destination positions. Your selected-side position template maps this exact row to U′.

One-minute cube practice

  1. Hold an empty F2L slot at front-right or front-left.
  2. Find one pair that fails a check. Say: position, corner orientation, or edge readiness.
  3. Say the smallest action before touching the cube: named U setup, switch pair, or one normalization.
  4. Make at most that action. Stop, then re-read from pair identity to the two-part green light.
