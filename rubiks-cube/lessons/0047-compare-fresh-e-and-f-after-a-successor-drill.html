Compare Fresh E/F after a successor drill

Turn a five-repetition F2L run into one justified next action—by comparing two new, like-for-like attempts.

The one thing to learn

After a fixed five-repetition successor run, make two fresh comparable attempts: Fresh E and Fresh F. For each, identify the first mismatch in the F2L workflow. The verdict comes from the pair, not from the five-run score or one promising attempt.

Decision rule: if E and F both move past the successor target, retain the drill. If either repeats that target, refine or slow it. If both share the same later first mismatch, retain the proven cue and make that later stage the next narrow target. If conditions differ, there is no verdict—repair the evidence.

A fresh attempt is not a sixth or seventh row of the five-run log. It is a new test of the fixed successor specification.

Read the evidence in order

  1. Run Fresh E under the written condition. Name its earliest mismatch.
  2. Reset to the same condition and run Fresh F. Name its earliest mismatch.
  3. Check comparability before interpreting either result.
  4. Compare each first mismatch with the successor target, not with a feeling of smoothness.
  5. Choose exactly one verdict: retain, refine/slow, target a shared later stage, or collect repaired evidence.
