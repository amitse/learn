Rubik’s Cube · F2L · under 10 minutes

Choose the next F2L action after a fresh read

After one controlled recovery, your old plan is gone. Learn the single decision that comes next: choose a named setup only when the new positions make it viable; otherwise normalize once—or work on a ready pair.

The fresh-read fork

Do not choose from memory of the old picture. Re-identify the corner, matching edge, centre-defined slot, and empty workspace. Then choose exactly one action based on what you can see now.
Ready pair?Insert it.
Viable positions?Name one U setup.
Neither?Normalize once, then re-read.

What “viable” means

For a selected right-side workspace, the basic destination positions are UFR + UF; for left, they are UFL + UF. A 0, U, U2, or U′ setup is justified only when the corner and matching edge have the same positional offset as one of those destination pictures.

This is only a position claim. After the one named setup, you still need the green light: white faces the selected side, and the edge at UF has its front sticker matching the front centre.

Read → choose: three outcomes

Outcome A — authorization

Your blue-red pair is at the right destination, white faces Right, and the edge’s front sticker matches the front centre. Action: insert once with R U R′. No recovery or U setup is needed.

Outcome B — positional route

The pair has the right-side position template, but is one U turn away from UFR + UF. Action: say U, turn it once, then perform the full green-light check. Do not append the trigger automatically.

Outcome C — no positional route

The corner-edge offset cannot reach a basic destination with any U turn, or the corner is white-up / the edge is unready. Action: if no other pair is green-lit, make one normalization trigger through the empty slot, then start a new read. Do not cycle U in hope.

Decision drill

Choose the next action, not the whole solve. Feedback is immediate.

1. After re-reading, a different unsolved pair is fully green-lit. The original recovered pair is not. What is least disruptive?

2. The selected pair has a viable left-side position template that requires U′ to reach UFL + UF. What is the next action?

3. The target corner is white-up. Its matching edge is not in a viable position template. No other pair is ready. What is next?

Three-minute cube drill

  1. After any normalization, take your hands off the cube for one beat.
  2. Say: “pair, slot, readiness, route.” Find the corner, matching edge, centre-defined empty slot, then classify the route.
  3. Commit to one label only: insert, named U setup, or normalize.
  4. After a named setup or normalization, inspect again. The action never carries insertion permission forward by itself.
