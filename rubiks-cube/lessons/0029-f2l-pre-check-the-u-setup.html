Pre-check whether a U setup can work

Today’s win: before turning U, use the corner and edge positions to reject a setup that can never reach your chosen F2L picture.

One idea: U turns move both pieces together

After Lesson 28, choose the working side from the corner: white Right → front-right; white Left → front-left. A U turn then carries every U-layer position around the top together. It cannot change the positional relationship between your target corner and target edge.

Fast filter: if their current positions do not appear in a row below, no choice of 0, U, U2, or U′ will place both in the basic picture at once. Do not turn; re-read or normalize instead.

The 25-second decision loop

Read and choose. Name the pair. Use the white sticker to select Right/front-right or Left/front-left. A White-Up corner still needs normalization.
Compare positions. Find the row for the selected side. If your corner-edge positions do not match a row, reject every U candidate.
Turn only the matching candidate. Use the U amount that brings that row to the goal picture. This is one deliberate setup, not a trial sequence.
Demand the green light. At the goal position, check white faces the selected side and the edge at UF has its front sticker matching the front centre. Only then trigger.
Important: a matching row only passes the position filter. The corner’s sticker direction and edge-centre check can still fail. If either fails, do not force the trigger.

One deliberate rep

Pick an unsolved U-layer F2L pair. Say: “Right (or Left) selected; corner is ___; edge is ___.” Compare once with the correct table before touching U. If it has no row, use a controlled normalization route—not random U turns.
