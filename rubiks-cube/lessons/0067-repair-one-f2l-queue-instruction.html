Repair one F2L queue instruction

You can already audit a queue line. Now make it usable again—by changing only the first failed clause from its source row.

Today's one skill

Repair, do not rewrite. Compare the proposed line to its selected card row in order: classification → scope → one action. Stop at the first mismatch and replace only that part. The rest of a correct line stays intact.

The three repair moves

1
Wrong label? Restore the row’s classification. FRESH EVIDENCE permits a matching replacement attempt only; ACT permits its approved drill only.
2
Right label, wrong scope? Copy the first missing or changed scope field: target pair, empty workspace, retained cue, or checkpoint.
3
Right label and scope, too many actions? Keep the row’s one permitted action and delete the extra action. Do not repair a later clause before an earlier one.

Worked repair: stop at scope

Selected Row C · FRESH EVIDENCE
Target: green-orange pair. Workspace: front-left empty slot. Cue: say “white left; edge matches front centre” before the trigger. Checkpoint: immediately before the trigger. Permitted action: collect one matching replacement Fresh E attempt.
Proposed line
“Collect one replacement Fresh E attempt for the green-orange pair with a front-right empty slot; say the cue before the trigger and record the pre-trigger checkpoint.”

Classification passes. The first failure is scope: front-right changed the workspace. The cue, checkpoint, and one replacement action already match—leave them alone.

Repaired line: “Collect one replacement Fresh E attempt for the green-orange pair with a front-left empty slot; say the cue before the trigger and record the pre-trigger checkpoint.”

Quick check

Selected Row A · ACT: approved action: “Run five slow named-U-setup reads for the blue-red pair with a front-right empty slot; checkpoint at destination positions.”

Proposed line: “Run five slow named-U-setup reads for the blue-red pair with a front-right empty slot; check destination positions, then collect Fresh F.” What is the minimal repair?

Do it now

Take one failed queue line from your card. Put the source row beside it and say:

row label → four scope fields → one action → first failure

Edit only that failure. Then do the repaired one-line instruction on the cube and record the stated checkpoint. If the source row itself is unclear, ask your teacher before turning.
