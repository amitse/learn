📋 Your Complete Method — All 7 Steps

This is your full solve pipeline. Every step, every algorithm you need:

1

White Cross

Place the 4 white edges on the white face, aligned to their centre colours. Intuitive — no fixed algorithm.

2

White Corners (Layer 1)

Insert the 4 white corners. Trigger: R U R' U' (right-trigger), repeated until corner slots in.

3

Second Layer Edges (F2L beginner)

Insert the 4 middle-layer edges. Two cases:

  • Edge goes right: U R U' R' U' F' U F
  • Edge goes left: U' L' U L U F U' F'
4

Yellow Cross (2-Look OLL step 1)

Make a yellow cross on top. Use F R U R' U' F'. Apply until cross appears (up to 3×).

5

Orient Yellow Corners (2-Look OLL step 2)

Sune: R U R' U R U2 R'  |  Anti-Sune: R U2 R' U' R U' R'

Identify your OLL case; apply once or twice until all yellow faces up.

6

Permute Yellow Corners (2-Look PLL step 1)

Find a matching corner pair → put it at back → Y-Perm: F R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R F'

7

Permute Yellow Edges (2-Look PLL step 2)

Solved edge at back. Front sticker goes left → Ua: R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2

Front sticker goes right → Ub: R2 U R U R' U' R' U' R' U R'

    🧠 Strategy for Your First Solve

    1. Slow down at each step boundary — pause, identify your case, then execute.
    2. White cross first. Try to get it in ≤ 4 moves per edge — think ahead before turning.
    3. Check before each F2L insertion — is the edge going left or right? Pick the right trigger.
    4. For OLL — count stickers, don't guess. Identify the Sune/Anti-Sune case first.
    5. For PLL — find the matching corner pair. Rotate the whole cube (y), not the U layer.
    6. If you get stuck — undo a few moves, re-identify, try again. Never restart from scratch unless completely lost.
