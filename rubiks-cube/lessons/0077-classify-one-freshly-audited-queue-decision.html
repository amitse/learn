Rubik's Cube · Lesson 77 · under 10 minutes

Classify one freshly audited queue decision

After a fresh audit, make only the status call: can this one decision guide the next cube action, or does it need one minimal repair first?

Today’s one skill

Classify an audited F2L queue decision as ACTION-READY or MINIMAL REPAIR NEEDED. Do not diagnose the cube, rewrite the decision, or execute it during classification.

The classification gate

All four audit gates passed? Literal checkpoint evidence, copied ACT-row permission, all four scope fields, and exactly one action.
Yes → ACTION-READY. It may direct its one stated cube-side action.
No → MINIMAL REPAIR NEEDED. Preserve the decision; repair only the first failed clause, then re-audit from the beginning.

The label is not a verdict on your solve. It is only a verdict on whether this written evidence-to-action unit is safe to use.

Classify this audited decision

Valid checkpoint log
Rep 4 — immediately before trigger: corner white faces Right; matching edge’s front sticker matches the front centre.
Selected ACT row
Run five slow green-light reads.
Decision after audit
“Because Rep 4 showed white facing Right and the edge matching the front centre immediately before trigger, run five slow green-light reads for the blue-red pair in a front-right empty slot, saying ‘white right; edge matches front centre’ before trigger.”
Classification: ACTION-READY
Its evidence is literal; its permission is copied; pair, workspace, cue/timing, and checkpoint are present; and it names one action only. The next move is the stated five-read drill—not an added insertion.

Contrast: a decision that is not ready

“Because Rep 4 showed white facing Right and the edge matching the front centre immediately before trigger, run five slow green-light reads for the blue-red pair in a front-left empty slot, saying ‘white right; edge matches front centre’ before trigger.”

MINIMAL REPAIR NEEDED The evidence and permission look fine, but the workspace differs from the source scope. Repair only front-left to front-right; then re-audit all four gates. Do not silently call it ready because the rest reads well.

Quick check

A fresh audit found that a decision has literal evidence, copied permission, all four scope fields, and one action. What is the correct classification?

Do it now

Put one freshly audited decision beside its log and ACT row. State its label out loud: all four pass → ACTION-READY, or first failure → MINIMAL REPAIR NEEDED. If it needs repair, name only the first failed clause and stop; ask your teacher to check a borderline classification with you.

Sources & further reading
