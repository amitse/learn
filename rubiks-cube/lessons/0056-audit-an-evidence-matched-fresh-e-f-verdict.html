Rubik’s Cube · F2L evidence practice

Audit an evidence-matched Fresh E/F verdict

Before changing an F2L drill, test the written verdict against the two fresh attempts. A plausible sentence is not enough: its condition, evidence, and one action must agree.

⏱ 6–8 minutes · One skill: pass or repair a verdict audit

The three-check audit

Use the logs—not your impression of the solve—as the source of truth.

1 · ConditionDo target pair, empty workspace, retained cue and its timing, and checkpoint match?
2 · EvidenceDoes the sentence report the actual first mismatch(es), without inventing a cause?
3 · ActionIs there exactly one action, and is it the action those observations support?

Fail one check? Repair only that clause. Do not alter the recorded logs or widen the drill.

Action map

  1. Both clear the target → retain the drill.
  2. Either repeats the target → minimally refine or slow the drill.
  3. Both share a genuinely later first mismatch → begin one narrow successor drill.
  4. Any condition field drifts → collect matching replacement evidence; no drill verdict yet.

Worked audit

Shared condition
blue-red pair · empty front-right workspace · “white direction, edge-centre” cue before setup · pre-trigger green-light checkpoint

Fresh E
first mismatch: edge-centre check fails at the checkpoint
Fresh F
first mismatch: edge-centre check fails at the checkpoint
Because Fresh E and F both repeat an edge-centre failure at the pre-trigger green light, slow the recognition drill.

Pass. The conditions match, the evidence clause says exactly what both logs show, and “slow the recognition drill” is one matched action. It does not claim that the algorithm or your hands caused the miss.

Quiz 1: find the failed check

Fresh E and F have identical pair, workspace, cue timing, and checkpoint. Both first fail after an authorized trigger. Proposed verdict: “Because Fresh E and F first fail after the trigger, retain the green-light drill and start an execution drill.” What fails?

Quiz 2: audit condition before meaning

Fresh E uses the blue-red pair, an empty front-right workspace, the cue before setup, and a pre-trigger checkpoint. Fresh F uses the same fields except the cue begins after setup. Both report an edge-centre failure. Which verdict is valid now?

Condition → evidence → action. If the condition fails, stop there and replace evidence. If it passes, copy the first mismatch exactly. Then permit one—and only one—action from the map.

Take it to the cube

  1. Put a Fresh E/F pair beside its one-sentence verdict.
  2. Tick the four condition fields, including when the cue began.
  3. Underline the first mismatch named in each log; compare those words with the evidence clause.
  4. Circle one action. If you circle two, rewrite. If a field drifted, replace that attempt instead.

Ask your teacher to audit a verdict with you whenever two mismatches sound similar but occur at different workflow checkpoints.
