Repair one audited Fresh E/F verdict

An audit failure does not erase a cube attempt. Locate the first unsupported clause, change only that clause, and then decide whether the repaired verdict is ready to act on.

⏱ 6–8 minutes · One skill: minimally repair an audited verdict

The repair sequence

1 · LocateWas the failed audit check condition, evidence, or action?
2 · Replace one clauseCopy the logs or condition exactly. Do not rewrite the whole sentence.
3 · ClassifyACT only when all checks now pass. Otherwise choose FRESH EVIDENCE.

If a condition field drifted, do not repair it with wording. Replace the drifted attempt under the matching condition.

Worked repair: a condition error

Fresh E
blue-red · empty front-right · cue before setup · pre-trigger checkpoint
Fresh F
blue-red · empty front-right · cue after setup · pre-trigger checkpoint
Failed verdict: Because both attempts show an edge-centre failure, slow the recognition drill.

Here the evidence and action cannot be used yet: cue timing drifted. The minimal repair is not a prettier verdict.

Repair: Because Fresh F’s cue began after setup, collect a matching replacement attempt.

FRESH EVIDENCE. Copy “cue before setup” into the replacement plan; only then compare first mismatches.

Quiz 1: repair the smallest thing

Fresh E and F are fully comparable. Both clear the current target and their first mismatch is later, after an authorized trigger. A verdict says: “Because both are later, retain the drill and begin an execution drill.” Which repair is correct?

Quiz 2: when repair requires new evidence

Fresh E used the retained cue before setup. Fresh F used the same cue after setup. The verdict says both repeated a green-light failure. What is the minimal repair?

Condition drift → replace evidence. Otherwise, repair the first unsupported clause only: condition, then evidence, then action. A repaired sentence is ACT only when it has comparable attempts, accurate first-mismatch evidence, and exactly one matched action.

Take it to the cube

  1. Place one audited failed verdict beside Fresh E and F.
  2. Mark the first failed check: condition, evidence, or action.
  3. Rewrite just that clause. If condition drifted, write a replacement-attempt plan instead.
  4. Label the result ACT or FRESH EVIDENCE before making another turn.

Ask your teacher to check a repair whenever two clauses seem wrong: fix the earliest audit failure first.
