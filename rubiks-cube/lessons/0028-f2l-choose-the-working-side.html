Choose the working side from the corner

You have read a ready F2L corner on U. Today’s win: let its white sticker choose the side—right means front-right; left means front-left—before you rotate or plan a U setup.

One idea: direction chooses the slot side

With white down, a ready target corner has its white sticker facing a side, not Up. That direction tells you which of the two safe insertion pictures is available. It is a side cue, not a command to insert yet.

White faces RightUse the front-right working side. Rotate the whole cube (y as needed) so the target slot is front-right, then seek the right picture: corner at UFR, matching edge at UF, edge front sticker = front centre. Insert only when green with R U R′.
White faces LeftUse the front-left working side. Rotate the whole cube as needed so the target slot is front-left, then seek the mirror picture: corner at UFL, matching edge at UF, edge front sticker = front centre. Insert only when green with L′ U′ L.

The 20-second decision script

Name the pair. Read the corner’s two non-white colours, locate its matching edge, and identify the centre-defined target slot.
Read white. Is white facing Right, Left, or Up? Up is not a side choice: normalize through an empty slot and re-read.
Choose the side. Right → front-right. Left → front-left. Use a whole-cube y rotation to bring that empty target slot to the working side; do not use a U turn to choose a side.
Plan one U candidate. From that hold, test only one of 0, U, U2, or U′.
Demand green. Check the corner location/direction and the edge-centre match. Then use the matching trigger—or undo and re-read.

Why rotate the whole cube first?

A y rotation changes your viewpoint but preserves every piece’s relationship to the fixed centres. A U move changes where top-layer pieces sit. Separating them keeps the job clear: y chooses your workspace; U tests an alignment inside it.

Guardrail: White-Up does not mean “pick either side.” U turns cannot change a corner from White-Up to Right- or Left-facing. Normalize first, then start the script again.

One deliberate rep

On a cube or virtual cube, find any unsolved F2L pair with the corner on U. Say aloud: “pair, white direction, working side.” Rotate the whole cube to put that empty slot at the front. Only then name one U candidate and apply the two-part green-light check.

Ask your teacher a follow-up if a case feels ambiguous. Include the pair’s colours, where white faces, which side you chose, and whether the edge’s front sticker matched the front centre.
