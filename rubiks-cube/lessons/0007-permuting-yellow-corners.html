Lesson 7 · Rubik's Cube

Permuting Yellow Corners

PLL Step 1 — one algorithm, three cases, corners locked into position.

Where you are

Layers 1 and 2 are solved. Yellow cross formed. All four top-layer corners show yellow on top — OLL is complete.

The problem: the corners may be in the wrong positions. A red corner sticker sitting next to the orange centre, a green corner next to blue. The orientation is right, the slot is wrong. This step fixes that.

Corners only, edges later. The 4 top-layer edges will still look scrambled after this step — that's expected. Lesson 8 (the last step!) handles edges.

How to spot headlights

Hold the cube with yellow on top. Look at each side face. You're only interested in the top strip — the two corner sticker squares in the top-left and top-right of each face, right under the yellow layer.

If both top-corner stickers on a face show the same colour, that's a headlights bar. It means those two corners are correctly paired for that face's column.

The corner permutation algorithm

This performs a 3-cycle of the front-left, front-right, and back-left corners. The back-right corner is left untouched — that's where the headlights go.

Setup: rotate the whole cube (y rotation) so headlights face the back. Yellow on top. Then apply:

R U R′ F′ · R U R′ U′ · R′ F · R2 U′ R′

Source: Ruwix Beginner's Method · J Perm Beginner Tutorial

Breaking it into groups

GroupMovesWhat it does
R U R′ F′ Setup Right Trigger + F′ opens the cycle, creates space to move corners
R U R′ U′ Right Trigger The same move from Lesson 3. You already know this cold.
R′ F Undo setup Mirrors the F′ at the start — closes the bracket
R2 U′ R′ Close Drops the cycled corners into their correct slots

Memory hook: "Trigger-flip · Trigger-undo · Unflip · Close"

The Right Trigger (R U R′ U′) from Lesson 3 sits right in the middle. If you can execute the trigger reliably, you already know half of this algorithm.

Full procedure

  1. Hold: yellow up, white down. Layers 1 and 2 already solved.
  2. Scan all 4 side strips. Do all corners already match their adjacent centre? → Go to Lesson 8.
  3. No headlights on any face? Diagonal case. Apply the algorithm from any hold — don't worry about orientation yet. After one application, headlights will exist somewhere.
  4. Find headlights. Do a whole-cube y-rotation (or y2) to bring that headlight face to the back.
  5. Apply: R U R′ F′ · R U R′ U′ · R′ F · R2 U′ R′
  6. Check. Every top corner should now sit in its correct slot — matching the adjacent centre on each side. Corner permutation done.

After this step: All 4 yellow corners are oriented and in the right position. One step remains — Lesson 8 permutes the last layer edges, and the cube is solved.
