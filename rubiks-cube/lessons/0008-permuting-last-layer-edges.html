Where you are: OLL is done (full yellow top face). All 4 last-layer corners are correctly positioned (Lesson 7). The only things left are the 4 edges — they may still be in wrong positions. This step fixes them. After this, the cube is solved.

Step 1 — Diagnose the Edges

Hold yellow up. Look at the side-facing sticker of each top-ring edge piece — the sticker that faces a side face, not the yellow sticker on top. Compare it to the centre colour directly below it.

Edges matching their centre Case name What to do
4 Solved 🎉 The cube is complete. You're done.
1 3-cycle Put correct edge at back. Identify direction → apply Ua or Ub.
0 H-perm / Z-perm Apply Ua from any hold → you'll get a 3-cycle → finish with step 3–5.

⚠️ You can never have exactly 2 or 3 edges correct on a valid cube. If you count 2 or 3, recheck: you may have misidentified an edge sticker, or the corners from Lesson 7 aren't fully placed yet.

Step 2 — Identify the Cycle Direction (3-cycle case)

Rotate the whole cube (y or y') so the one correct edge is at the back. Now look at the front edge — where does it belong?

The front edge piece belongs to… Algorithm
The left slot Ua
The right slot Ub

The Two Algorithms

Ua perm — front edge goes LEFT
Ua
R U' R  U R U R  U' R' U' R2

Groups: R U' R · U R U R · U' R' U' R2. Rhythm: setup, clockwise surge, close with R2.

Ub perm — front edge goes RIGHT
Ub
R2 U R  U R' U' R'  U' R' U R'

Ub is the exact inverse of Ua — read Ua backwards, invert each move. Know one, derive the other.

What each perm does (view from above, yellow up)

Back edge stays fixed. The three remaining edges cycle:

Ua — front goes LEFT
Ub — front goes RIGHT

Full Procedure (5 Steps)

  1. Hold yellow up. Inspect the 4 top-ring edge pieces — their side-facing stickers.
  2. Count correct edges (sticker colour = centre colour below it).
    • 4 correct → solved! 🎉
    • 0 correct → apply Ua from any hold, then restart from step 2
    • 1 correct → continue to step 3
  3. Rotate the whole cube (y or y') to place the correct edge at the back.
  4. Identify direction: front edge belongs LEFT → Ua; belongs RIGHT → Ub.
  5. Apply Ua or Ub. All 4 edges will land in their correct positions.

✓ Maximum 2 algorithm applications from any scramble. The 0-correct case converts to a 3-cycle after one application, which then resolves in one more.
