Rubik’s Cube · F2L evidence practice

Audit a matching Fresh E/F plan

Before you practise, catch the one field that makes a written evidence plan non-comparable.

⏱ 6–8 minutes · One skill: pass or minimally repair a collection plan

The audit rule

A plan is ready only when both Fresh E and Fresh F state the same four condition fields. This is a pre-turn check: it protects your practice time from producing evidence that cannot answer its own question.

Compare fields, not wording. One changed field → repair that field before touching the cube.

PairSame centre-defined corner-edge pair.
WorkspaceSame type of empty working slot.
Retained cueSame proven cue, used from the start.
CheckpointSame boundary for recording the first mismatch.

Run the four-field audit

  1. Read E and F side by side. Underline pair, workspace, cue, and checkpoint in each.
  2. Compare one column at a time. Do not accept “basically the same.” Front-right and front-left are different workspace types.
  3. Pass or name one drift. If all four match, write ready to collect. If one differs, name only that field.
  4. Make the smallest repair. Copy the baseline field into the drifted line. Do not replace the pair, invent a new cue, or revise the drill.

Worked audit

Baseline from the evidence-needed verdict: blue-red pair · empty front-right workspace · cue “name white direction, then match edge to centre” · checkpoint = pre-trigger green light.

Fresh E
blue-red · empty front-right · say “white direction, edge-to-centre” before setup · pre-trigger green light

Fresh F
blue-red · empty front-right · say “white direction, edge-to-centre” after the U move · pre-trigger green light

Audit: the cue field drifted. “After the U move” is not the retained cue from the start. Repair Fresh F by moving the same cue to before setup; the other three fields already pass.

Quick check

Which proposed E/F plan passes the audit?

One-field repair

A plan says: Fresh E = green-orange pair, empty front-right, retained cue from the start, pre-trigger green light. Fresh F has the same pair, workspace, and cue—but records after the insertion trigger. What repair is valid?

Pocket audit: “E and F: same pair, workspace, cue, checkpoint?” If yes, collect. If no, circle the first drifted field and copy its baseline value into the plan. Then collect—not before.

Take it to the cube

  1. Take your latest matching collection plan, or write one from the four fields.
  2. Put E and F on separate lines and underline each field.
  3. Audit aloud: pair, workspace, cue, checkpoint.
  4. Repair a mismatch in writing before you run either Fresh attempt.

Ask your teacher to audit the two lines with you if any wording feels ambiguous.
