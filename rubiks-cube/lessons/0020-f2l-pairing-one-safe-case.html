Rubik’s Cube · Lesson 0020 · under 10 minutes

Pair one F2L case before you insert it

You can identify a pair and protect an empty working slot. Now learn one reliable relationship: a corner with white facing right, beside its matching edge at the front.

The one safe picture

Hold white down, yellow up, with your empty target slot at front-right. Put the target corner at UFR (upper-front-right) with its white sticker facing the right. Put its matching edge at UF (upper-front), with its front-facing colour matching the front centre.

Corner: white faces right
+
Matching edge: front colour matches front centre
This is a relationship, not just two locations. The corner and edge must be the same colour pair from Lesson 18, and the front centre tells you which side of the edge faces front.

Turn the picture into a pair

When that picture is ready above an empty front-right slot, execute the familiar right trigger:

R U R′

R opens the front-right working space.
U brings the edge into the corner’s path.
R′ closes the slot with the corner and edge together.

Do not try to reason through every sticker while turning. First make the visual picture; then execute this three-move chunk smoothly. Afterward, inspect: the corner and edge should now be joined in the front-right slot.

Setup rule: use U, not hope

In a real scramble the pieces will rarely begin in this exact picture. Use U, U′, or U2 to move top-layer pieces into position. If a U setup cannot create this picture without moving through a solved slot, choose another pair or rotate the cube so its empty slot is your working slot.

Today’s boundary: learn one case cleanly. A mismatched edge, a non-empty working slot, or white not facing right means: do not force R U R′. Reset the picture first.

One deliberate rep

Try this on a real or virtual cube: make your cross, select one unsolved pair, and name its slot. Put that empty slot front-right. Use only U turns until the corner is at UFR with white on the right and its matching edge is at UF with the front colour matching the front centre. Say “picture ready,” then do R U R′. Inspect the slot before doing anything else.

A clean rep is progress even if setup takes a minute. Repeat it a few times; speed comes after the visual picture is dependable. Ask your teacher a follow-up question if your pair does not join—describe where the white sticker and matching edge were before the trigger.
