Rubik's Cube · Lesson 0005

The Yellow Cross

Goal: Get all 4 yellow edge stickers facing up — completing the first step of solving the last layer (OLL Step 1).

1 Where You Are

Layers 1 and 2 are complete — white face down, two full layers solved. The top face (yellow) still needs work. The last layer is solved in two stages:

  • OLL — Orientation: Get all yellow stickers facing up.
  • PLL — Permutation: Move pieces into the right positions.

This lesson covers OLL Step 1: making the yellow cross. You only need the 4 yellow edge stickers to face up. Yellow corners are irrelevant at this stage — ignore them completely.

Keep white face DOWN for all remaining steps of the solve.

2 The 4 Cases

Look at the top face. Ignoring corners, the 4 edge positions can show yellow or not. You'll see exactly one of these four patterns:

Dot
0 yellow edges up
Only the centre is yellow
L-shape
2 adjacent edges up
Shown: back + left (correct setup)
Bar
2 opposite edges up
Shown: left + right (correct setup)
Cross ✓
4 edges up — done!
Skip to Lesson 6.
Yellow corners don't matter yet. Their stickers may face sideways or up — ignore them completely at this stage.
Why only 0, 2, or 4? Edges can only flip in pairs on a standard 3×3 cube. Having 1 or 3 yellow edges up is geometrically impossible on a correctly assembled cube.

3 One Algorithm — All Cases

A single 6-move algorithm solves all three starting cases. Apply it once (with the right setup) to get the cross.

The Yellow Cross Algorithm
F R U R′ U′ F′
FFront face clockwise
RRight face clockwise
UUp (yellow) face clockwise
R′Right face counter-clockwise
U′Up (yellow) face counter-clockwise
F′Front face counter-clockwise — undoes the opening F
Recognise the pattern: The middle 4 moves — R U R′ U′ — are the Right Trigger from Layer 1. The F and F′ wrap around it.

4 Setup + Apply

For each case, orient the cube before applying the algorithm:

Dot
No setup needed — any orientation works. Apply F R U R′ U′ F′ once. You'll now see either an L-shape or a Bar. Identify which case you have, set it up correctly, and apply once more → Cross.
L-shape
Rotate the whole cube (U turns only) until the two yellow edges are at the Back and Left — the elbow of the L points toward back-left, the gap faces front-right. Apply F R U R′ U′ F′ once → Cross.
Bar
Rotate the whole cube (U turns only) until the bar is horizontal — yellow edges pointing left and right. Apply F R U R′ U′ F′ once → Cross.

Worst case is 2 applications. If you start with a Dot and the algorithm gives you an L or Bar, you're always one more application (with correct setup) away from the Cross.

6 Practice

The algorithm will feel stiff at first. Here's how to drill it:

  1. Solve layers 1 and 2.
  2. Look at the top face — identify your case (Dot / L / Bar / Cross).
  3. Orient if needed (L → back-left · Bar → horizontal).
  4. Apply F R U R′ U′ F′. Check. Repeat if coming from a Dot.
