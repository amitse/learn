Run one controlled F2L recovery

You can classify a failed green light. Now turn that judgment into one complete, calm recovery cycle: choose one action, execute it once, and earn the next insertion picture by reading again.

The one-cycle rule

A recovery ends at a re-read, not an insertion. When a pair fails its green light and no other pair is ready, protect an empty working slot, make one normalization trigger through it, then begin again with pair identity. Do not append a second trigger because the picture is unfamiliar.

Walk one concrete recovery

Starting observation

Your blue-red pair is selected. Front-right is an empty blue-red working slot. The corner and edge have reached UFR + UF, but the edge’s front sticker does not match the front centre. No other unsolved pair is fully green-lit.

  1. Name the diagnosis: edge-readiness failure. Locations are useful, but the edge-centre check fails, so R U R′ is not an insertion.
  2. Commit the boundary: use the empty front-right slot only. Completed pairs are not workspace.
  3. Normalize once: perform R U R′ through that empty slot. Here it is a recovery trigger, not an insertion trigger.
  4. Put your hands down and re-read: find the blue-red corner, its matching edge, and the empty slot again. Only then choose a named U setup or an insertion if the full green light now exists.

The recovery’s success condition is not “the pair is solved.” It is “the cube is still legible and I have fresh observations.” That makes the next decision reliable.

What must not happen

Temptation

“The first normalization did not immediately make a right-facing picture, so I’ll do another trigger.”

Stop. The first trigger changed the relationship. A second one without a new read is guessing; it can turn a recoverable state into a confusing one. Re-identify the pair before every new plan.

Recovery decision drill

Pick the next action. Feedback is immediate.

1. The target pair is at destination positions, but its edge fails the front-centre check. Front-right is empty and no other pair is ready. What is the complete next step?

2. After one normalization, the target corner is on U but you cannot immediately name its partner’s position. What now?

3. During the re-read, a different unsolved pair is fully green-lit. The original pair is not. Which choice preserves the cube best?

Three-minute real-cube drill

  1. Leave one F2L slot empty at front-right. Find a pair with an unready edge or white-up corner.
  2. Say aloud: “[pair colours], [failure], one recovery through front-right.”
  3. Make exactly one R U R′. Remove your hands for one beat.
  4. Re-read all four things: corner identity, matching edge, centre-defined slot, and both green-light checks. Do not insert unless the new read authorizes it.
