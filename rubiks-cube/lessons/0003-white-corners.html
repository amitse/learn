White Corners

Complete Layer 1 — insert all 4 white corners

✅ What you'll accomplish

After this lesson you can finish the entire white layer from scratch. You'll learn the Right Trigger — the single most useful algorithm in beginner cubing — and how to apply it across three situations.

Setup

You've solved the white cross. The next step is inserting the 4 white corner pieces into the gaps between the cross edges. Once they're all in, Layer 1 is complete.

Hold white DOWN for this entire step. Keep the white face on the bottom (D face) throughout. Never flip the cube — you'll only ever turn the top layer and the right or front faces.

Where does each corner belong?

Each white corner has 3 coloured stickers: white plus two side colours. Its home slot is where those two side colours match the two adjacent centre pieces.

Example: A corner with white + green + red stickers belongs in the front-right-bottom slot — between the green centre (front) and red centre (right).

Centres never move. They are your colour compass for the whole solve — always match to centres, never to edges.

The Right Trigger: R U R′ U′

This 4-move sequence is the most important algorithm in the beginner method. The cubing community calls it the Right Trigger (sometimes "Sexy Move"). You'll use it for corners, middle-layer edges, and beyond — learn it cold.

Right Trigger
R U R′ U′
Say it aloud: "Right — Up — Right-back — Up-back"

What it does: it briefly pulls the front-right corner out of the bottom layer, shifts the top layer, then returns the corner — but in a new orientation. Applied to a corner above its slot, this either inserts it directly or re-orients it for a second attempt.

The 3 cases

For each white corner:

  1. Use U turns to move the corner directly above its target slot (front-right-top = UFR position). Match the corner's two side colours to the two centres below.
  2. Look at where the white sticker is pointing. There are 3 possibilities:

💡 Case 3 tip: Apply all 12 moves in one smooth sequence without pausing. R U R' U' × 3 will cycle the corner through two intermediate states before landing it correctly on the third repetition.

Corner stuck in the bottom layer?

Sometimes you'll find a white corner already in the bottom layer — but in the wrong slot, or twisted the wrong way. You can't insert it directly from there.

Extract it first:

  1. Position the stuck corner at the front-right-bottom slot (rotate the whole cube — holding white still down — so the problem corner is at front-right).
  2. Apply R U R′ — this lifts the corner into the top layer.
  3. Now use U turns to bring it above its correct slot, identify the case, and insert normally.

Full process — layer 1 from white cross

  1. Keep white DOWN. Always.
  2. Scan the top layer for a white corner. Spot its two side colours.
  3. Use U turns to bring the corner above its target slot (match side colours to centres below).
  4. Identify which case applies (right / front / up) and execute the algorithm.
  5. If a corner is stuck in the bottom layer → extract with R U R′, then go back to step 2.
  6. Repeat for all 4 corners. White layer done.
