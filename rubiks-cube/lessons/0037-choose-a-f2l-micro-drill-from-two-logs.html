Rubik’s Cube · under 10 minutes

Choose one F2L micro-drill from two logs

Your goal is a repeatable solve, not lucky turns. Two attempts are enough to select the next small practice target.

The one thing to learn

Do not drill every part of F2L after a miss. Compare the first mismatch in two observation logs. Drill the earliest category that repeats.

Decision rule: repeated mismatch before destination positions → planning drill. Repeated mismatch after destination positions but before a trigger → green-light drill. Repeated mismatch after a verified green light and trigger → execution drill.

Read the evidence

Attempt A
Plan: right side, U setup.
First mismatch: after U, corner/edge are not at UFR + UF.
Attempt B
Plan: right side, U setup.
First mismatch: after U, corner/edge are not at UFR + UF.

Both failures happen before the destination picture. The right micro-drill is not a trigger drill: it is pair/slot/side/setup planning.

  1. Choose one pair and name its matching edge and centre-defined slot.
  2. Choose the side from the white sticker (Right or Left).
  3. Predict one setup: 0, U, U2, or U′.
  4. Turn it, then stop and check positions only. Repeat 5 times; no insertion trigger.
