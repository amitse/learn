Rubik’s Cube · F2L evidence practice

Audit one action-ready verdict

Before acting on an F2L practice conclusion, find the first clause that outruns the two Fresh logs. Repair only that clause.

⏱ 6–8 minutes · One skill: audit an apparent ACT verdict in the correct order

The audit order is the skill

An action-ready verdict needs all three checks to pass, in order:

1 · CONDITION
Fresh E and F must match on target pair, empty workspace type, retained cue including timing, and checkpoint. Any drift means FRESH EVIDENCE; stop.
2 · EVIDENCE
The sentence must state the recorded first mismatch literally. Do not smuggle in a cause such as “rushing” or “bad grip.”
3 · ACTION
It names exactly one action that follows: retain, minimally refine/slow, begin one shared later-stage drill, or collect matching evidence.

The first failed check is the repair target. A good action cannot rescue unmatched evidence.

Fresh E and F condition

Fresh F: first mismatch = edge-centre check fails

Proposed verdict:

Because Fresh E and F both first fail the edge-centre check, the grip is wrong, so slow the green-light recognition drill.

Audit: condition passes; the first evidence phrase is accurate; then the verdict adds an unobserved cause. The smallest repair deletes only “the grip is wrong, so”.

Because Fresh E and F both first fail the edge-centre check, slow the green-light recognition drill.

Now it is ACT: comparable logs, literal evidence, one matched action.

Quiz 1: locate the first failure

Fresh E uses an empty front-right workspace. Fresh F uses an empty front-left workspace. Their draft says: “Because Fresh E and F both first fail the edge-centre check, slow the recognition drill.” What must the audit return?

Quiz 2: make the smallest repair

All four condition fields match.
Fresh E: first mismatch = destination positions missed
Fresh F: first mismatch = destination positions missed

Draft: “Because Fresh E and F both miss destination positions because the cube is awkward, slow the named-U-setup planning drill.” Which repair is minimal?

90-second cube-side audit

  1. Take one current Fresh E/F verdict that looks ready to use.
  2. Underline its four condition fields in the two logs. If one differs, write FRESH EVIDENCE and copy the baseline field into a replacement-attempt plan.
  3. If they match, underline the literal first mismatch and circle the single action. Cross out the first extra cause or second action—nothing else.

Ask your teacher to audit the repaired sentence if you cannot tell whether a phrase is a recorded observation or a story about why it happened.
