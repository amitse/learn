# 0021 — F2L Pairing: The Mirror Case

**Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Lesson:** `0021-f2l-pairing-mirror-case.html`

## What was taught

- The left-front mirror of the safe F2L picture from Lesson 20.
- With white down and an **empty front-left** working slot, place the target corner at **UFL** with white facing left.
- Place its matching edge at **UF**, with its front-facing sticker matching the front centre.
- Execute the Left Trigger: **`L′ U′ L`** to join and insert that prepared pair.
- Select a safe side deliberately: right-facing picture + empty front-right uses `R U R′`; left-facing picture + empty front-left uses `L′ U′ L`.

## Key non-obvious insights

- This is a geometric mirror, not a separate F2L system: the workspace, white-sticker direction, U direction, and trigger all reverse together.
- Pair identity remains the same colour-chain process from Lesson 18; the change is only the prepared relationship and chosen working slot.
- A whole-cube `y` rotation chooses which empty slot is at the front, while U turns prepare top-layer pieces. Neither is permission to run a trigger through a protected pair.

This grows recognition into flexible pairing without introducing a large case list.
