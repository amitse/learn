# 0064 — Classify an Audited Mixed Triage Card

**Date:** 2026-10-19  
**Lesson:** `0064-classify-an-audited-mixed-triage-card.html`

## What was taught

- Classify each repaired mixed-verdict card line independently as **ACT** only when its condition fields are comparable, its note is literal, and it gives exactly one evidence-matched instruction.
- Classify a line with a remaining drifted condition as **FRESH EVIDENCE** and treat its matching replacement attempt as its entire next action.
- Turn an audited card into a safe queue by selecting one row-local action rather than transferring evidence or combining drills across rows.

## Key non-obvious insights

- An action-ready line and an evidence-needed line can coexist on the same card; neither changes the status of the other.
- A valid ACT row is not permission to interpret or alter a FRESH EVIDENCE row with a similar-looking mismatch.
- Selecting one instruction protects attribution: a replacement attempt collects evidence, while an ACT instruction tests one narrow practice change.
