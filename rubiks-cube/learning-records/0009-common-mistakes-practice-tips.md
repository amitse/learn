# 0009 — Common Mistakes & Practice Tips

**Date:** 2026-06-26
**Lesson:** `0009-common-mistakes-practice-tips.html`

## What was taught

- Stage-by-stage catalogue of the most common mistakes in the beginner layer-by-layer method, with concrete fixes for each
- Orientation errors (wrong face held as "front", wrong hold between Sune applications) identified as the root cause of most unexpected algorithm outcomes
- 0-correct-edges state confirmed as a valid case, not a sign of a mistake in Stage 6
- U-moves vs whole-cube rotations (y): U must NOT be used during Stages 6–7 to reposition — always use full-cube rotations
- Practice routine: stage drills, full slow solves, timed solves, algorithm recall, and the "dirty dozen" drill
- Recovery strategy: stages are independent; a failed last-layer step does not require rebuilding from Stage 1

## Key non-obvious insights

- The single most common failure mode across all stages is **misreading orientation** — applying the correct algorithm from the wrong hold
- Between consecutive Sune applications (Stage 5), the cube must NOT be repositioned — many learners instinctively spin it to check corners and accidentally change the outcome
- Stage 3 (second-layer edges): the U-alignment move is the most-skipped step, causing solved edges to get knocked out
- **Trust the algorithm**: if a result looks wrong, the diagnosis step was wrong — not the algorithm itself
- Recovery via Ua×1 from a 0-correct-edge case is a universal fix; memorising this removes a major source of restart anxiety

## Zone of proximal development — next

After completing 5 full unaided solves:

- **Finger tricks** — physical technique improvements for common moves (R U R′) without learning new algorithms
- **Look-ahead practice** — planning the next stage while executing the current one
- **Intro to CFOP / F2L** — collapse Stages 1–3 into a more efficient "First Two Layers" method
