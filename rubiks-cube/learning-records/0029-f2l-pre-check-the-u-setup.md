# 0029 — F2L: Pre-check the U Setup

**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Lesson:** `0029-f2l-pre-check-the-u-setup.html`

## What was taught

- Once the ready corner has selected the right or left working side, a U turn moves the target corner and edge together and preserves their positional relationship on U.
- The learner can use the right-side or left-side position template to decide whether a single U setup candidate can reach the known F2L picture before turning.
- A template match is only a positional filter: the corner white-direction check and edge-centre check remain required before using the short trigger.
- A non-matching position pair means U turns cannot make the basic picture; the appropriate response is to re-read or normalize through an empty slot, not cycle U randomly.

## Key non-obvious insights

- The four U candidates are not four independent guesses: their common rotation keeps the corner-edge offset fixed, so a single table row determines whether any candidate is viable.
- Separating positional viability from orientation avoids a new mistake: treating a promising location as permission to insert.
- This filter reduces unproductive turning while preserving the earlier workflow: identify pair → choose side → test viable setup → green-light check → trigger.
