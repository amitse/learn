# 0012 — Intuitive F2L (First Two Layers)

**Date:** 2026-07-04
**Lesson:** `0012-intuitive-f2l.html`

## What was taught

- **F2L** defined: collapsing the 3 separate beginner stages (white cross, white corners, second-layer edges) into one unified "First Two Layers" stage
- The **corner-edge pair** as the fundamental unit of F2L — 4 pairs in total, one per slot
- How to identify a pair: corner's two *non-white* stickers define the pair partner edge's colours exactly
- **4 slots** = the 4 corner-edge positions in the bottom two layers, named by their two adjacent side-centre colours
- The two trigger moves that do all the work: **Right Trigger** (`R U R'`) and **Left Trigger** (`L' U' L`)
- **Pair insertion method**: get both pieces to the top layer → use U moves to align → execute trigger
- The **4 cases** encountered in real solves:
  1. Both pieces on top → direct insertion
  2. Corner on top, edge in middle → eject edge first
  3. Corner in wrong slot → eject with trigger, then insert
  4. Both pieces in wrong slots → eject one at a time
- **Ejecting** a stuck piece: one trigger at the offending slot lifts the piece to U without disturbing other solved slots
- No new algorithms required — only the two triggers from beginner finger-tricks practice

## Key non-obvious insights

- White is never a shared colour in an F2L pair — it's the corner's "floor" sticker, not an identifier
- Ejecting a piece from one slot does not affect other already-solved slots — each slot is isolated, eliminating restart anxiety
- F2L is slower before it's faster: beginners will feel the approach is less efficient at first because they're searching for pairs instead of following a step-by-step checklist. The efficiency comes with recognition speed, not from memorising new algorithms
- The entire OLL + PLL last layer procedure is completely unchanged — F2L only affects what happens before it
- Look-ahead (lesson 11) directly feeds F2L: while inserting pair N, glance for pair N+1's pieces on the U face

## Zone of proximal development — next

- **5 F2L solves drill**: time each solve, log which pair took longest to find — this reveals weak recognition patterns to target
- **Sub-3-minute target**: once solves are consistently complete, set a 3-minute target using look-ahead during F2L scanning
- **Intro to full CFOP (OLL algorithms)**: after fluent F2L, learning 2-look OLL shortcuts reduces last-layer step count significantly
