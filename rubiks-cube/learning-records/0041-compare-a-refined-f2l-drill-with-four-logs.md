# 0041 — Compare a Refined F2L Drill with Four Logs

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Lesson:** `0041-compare-a-refined-f2l-drill-with-four-logs.html`

## What was taught

- A refined F2L micro-drill is assessed with two baseline logs and two fresh logs.
- The target pair, empty working-slot type, and observation checkpoint must stay comparable; the one stated refinement is the only intentional change.
- Retain the refinement only when both fresh comparable logs move the first mismatch later or remove it; condition drift makes the comparison inconclusive.

## Key non-obvious insights

- Two successful fresh attempts are not evidence for the refinement if either changes the pair, workspace, or diagnostic boundary from the baselines.
- A later mismatch is evidence that the original bottleneck moved, not a reason to restart a solve or keep drilling the old stage indefinitely.
- A four-log comparison preserves the mission’s recovery focus by distinguishing a controlled improvement from a lucky attempt.
