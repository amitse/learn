# 0042 — Write One Minimal F2L Drill Refinement

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Lesson:** `0042-write-one-minimal-f2l-drill-refinement.html`

## What was taught

- An inconclusive four-log comparison must be diagnosed as either condition drift or a repeated earliest mismatch before changing a drill.
- When pair, empty workspace type, or observation checkpoint drifted, the next action is to collect a matching fresh log—not introduce another refinement.
- When the same earliest mismatch repeats under comparable conditions, refine one drill feature only and specify two new comparable fresh logs.

## Key non-obvious insights

- A replacement log after condition drift is evidence repair, not a failed drill or a new skill intervention.
- A successful fresh attempt cannot establish improvement when the second fresh log uses a different observation boundary.
- Minimal change preserves attribution: it makes the next verdict about the cue or pace rather than several simultaneous changes.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L: classify a completed refined-drill cycle and choose its next action.** Given four comparable logs after a minimal refinement, distinguish retain, one further refinement, and a newly visible later bottleneck without widening the drill.
