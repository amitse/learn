# 0051 — Classify Repaired Fresh E/F Verdicts

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Lesson:** `0051-classify-repaired-fresh-e-f-verdicts.html`

## What was taught

- A repaired Fresh E/F verdict is action-ready only when the attempts are comparable and its single action matches their recorded first mismatches.
- A repair that identifies condition drift is evidence-needed: collect matching fresh attempts before altering the drill.
- A comparable pair where either attempt repeats the old target supports a minimal refinement or slower drill, even if the other attempt reaches a later stage.

## Key non-obvious insights

- A correct written repair can still be non-actionable; “collect matching evidence” protects the controlled practice condition rather than authorizing a new drill.
- Different apparent outcomes do not invalidate comparable evidence automatically—the earliest repeated mismatch governs the next narrow action.
- Labelling a conclusion ACT or FRESH EVIDENCE separates a defensible practice decision from a merely plausible story.
