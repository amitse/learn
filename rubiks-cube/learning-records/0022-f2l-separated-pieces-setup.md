# 0022 — F2L: Set Up Separated Pieces

**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Lesson:** `0022-f2l-separated-pieces-setup.html`

## What was taught

- When a target F2L corner and its matching edge are both on U but are separated, use a `U` or `U′` setup turn before inserting.
- First choose and protect an empty working slot at the front with a whole-cube `y` rotation.
- The setup’s destination is one of two known pairing pictures: right-facing at UFR → `R U R′`, or left-facing at UFL → `L′ U′ L`.
- Confirm both the corner’s white-sticker direction and the edge’s front-centre match before committing to a trigger.

## Key non-obvious insights

- A U setup changes top-layer relationships while preserving completed F2L slots; a trigger through a protected slot is the real risk.
- Flexible F2L starts with turning an unfamiliar position into a familiar picture, rather than memorising an insertion for every visual arrangement.
- If a U move does not yield a confirmed safe picture, stop and inspect; forcing a trigger makes diagnosis harder.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L: separate then pair.** Teach one controlled case where a corner and its edge must first be separated deliberately with a setup move, then rejoined into a safe right- or left-facing picture. This adds recovery without a large case list.
