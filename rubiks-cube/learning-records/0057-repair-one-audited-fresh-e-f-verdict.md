# 0057 — Repair One Audited Fresh E/F Verdict

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Lesson:** `0057-repair-one-audited-fresh-e-f-verdict.html`

## What was taught

- Repair a failed Fresh E/F verdict by identifying the first failed audit check—condition, evidence, or action—and changing only the clause that check requires.
- When the attempts are comparable, retain the accurate observation clause and replace an unsupported action with exactly one evidence-matched action.
- When a condition field drifts, wording cannot restore comparability: write a matching replacement-attempt plan and classify the verdict as **FRESH EVIDENCE**, not action-ready.

## Key non-obvious insights

- A verdict with two reasonable next steps still fails; a controlled practice conclusion selects one action.
- The earliest audit failure constrains the repair. An action cannot be repaired from evidence whose condition fields do not match.
- A minimal verdict repair preserves useful observations and avoids inventing causes, changing algorithms, or widening the target pair.
