# 0020 — F2L Pairing: One Safe Case

**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Lesson:** `0020-f2l-pairing-one-safe-case.html`

## What was taught

- One specific F2L pairing picture above an empty front-right working slot: the target corner at UFR with white facing right, and its matching edge at UF with the front-facing sticker matching the front centre.
- Once the picture is prepared, the familiar `R U R′` right trigger joins and inserts the pair into the front-right slot.
- U-layer setup turns are used to create the picture while preserving completed F2L slots; a trigger should never be forced through a solved slot.

## Key non-obvious insights

- Pair identity alone is not enough: the corner and edge also need a useful *relationship* before a short trigger can join them.
- Fixed centres validate the edge orientation: matching the edge’s front sticker to the front centre makes the setup readable rather than guesswork.
- A failed setup is a signal to reset the working slot or choose another pair, not a reason to damage an already completed pair.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L mirror pairing case:** learn the left-front mirror of this same picture—white facing left with the matching edge oriented at the front—then use `L′ U′ L`. This adds one symmetric option without introducing a large F2L case list.
