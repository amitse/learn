# 0002 — The White Cross

**Date:** 2026-06-11
**Lesson:** `0002-white-cross.html`

## What was taught

- The white cross is step 1 of the layer-by-layer beginner method
- A completed white cross requires **4 white edge pieces** aligned with their adjacent side centres — not just a cross shape
- Centres never move; they are the fixed colour compass for the whole solve

## The Daisy Method

Two-stage approach:
1. **Build the daisy** — move all 4 white edges to the U face (yellow centre up) with white stickers facing upward, regardless of alignment
2. **Match and lock** — for each petal, rotate U until side colour aligns above matching centre, then execute **F2** to drop it into the white cross

## Key cases for building the daisy

| Piece location | Technique |
|---|---|
| Top layer, white sideways | U turns + F to orient |
| Middle layer (E layer) | F to bring up, then U turns |
| Bottom layer, white down | F2 to flip to top |
| Bottom layer, white sideways | F U R U' |

## Non-obvious insight

The daisy stage is intentionally exploratory — no rigid algorithm needed. Students should feel free to experiment and recover if they displace a petal. The lock stage (U + F2) is the only mechanical step.
