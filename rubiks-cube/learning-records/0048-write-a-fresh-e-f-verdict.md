# 0048 — Write a Fresh E/F Verdict

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Lesson:** `0048-write-a-fresh-e-f-verdict.html`

## What was taught

- A Fresh E/F verdict is one sentence that connects two comparable first-mismatch observations to exactly one next action.
- If both fresh logs clear the tested target, retain its cue; if either repeats it, minimally refine or slow the drill; if both share one genuinely later mismatch, retain the cue and target that later stage; condition drift requires matching fresh evidence.
- A useful verdict names the evidence and action but does not introduce an unsupported explanation, new algorithm, or changed practice condition.

## Key non-obvious insights

- The five-repetition run is not the verdict; Fresh E/F are the planned post-run test that makes the next action defensible.
- “Both improved” is incomplete: the sentence must distinguish between simply retaining a drill and advancing to a shared later workflow bottleneck.
- One evidence-backed action is stronger than a long account of possible causes; causes can be tested later under a controlled condition.

## Zone of proximal development — next
