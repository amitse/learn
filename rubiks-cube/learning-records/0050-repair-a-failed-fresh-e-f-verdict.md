# 0050 — Repair a Failed Fresh E/F Verdict

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Lesson:** `0050-repair-a-failed-fresh-e-f-verdict.html`

## What was taught

- A failed Fresh E/F verdict is repaired by locating the failed clause: comparable condition, accurate evidence statement, or evidence-matched single action.
- The repair must replace only the unsupported clause; it must not add a cause, new algorithm, changed target pair, or extra action.
- Comparable fresh logs support retaining after both clear the target, minimal refinement/slowing when either repeats it, a successor only after both share a genuinely later mismatch, and new evidence after condition drift.

## Key non-obvious insights

- A post-trigger explanation is unsupported when no authorized trigger occurred; the earliest mismatch bounds what the verdict can claim.
- Condition drift invalidates both a positive evidence claim and an action claim, even when both attempts appear successful.
- A small repair preserves the controlled practice condition and makes the following attempt interpretable.
