# 0066 — Audit One F2L Queue Instruction

**Date:** 2026-10-23  
**Lesson:** `0066-audit-one-f2l-queue-instruction.html`

## What was taught

- Audit a proposed one-line F2L queue instruction against its selected audited-card row in a fixed order: classification, scope, then one action.
- Preserve an **ACT** row’s approved drill, while a **FRESH EVIDENCE** row authorizes only one matching replacement attempt.
- Check that the row’s target pair, empty-workspace type, retained cue, and checkpoint travel together into the instruction.

## Key non-obvious insights

- Stop at the first audit failure: a later accurate clause cannot repair an incorrect classification or a borrowed condition.
- An instruction that combines a drill and replacement collection has crossed the one-action boundary even when both actions sound useful.
- Queue auditing protects the repeatable-solve goal because every cube attempt remains tied to one interpretable condition and action.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L: repair one failed queue instruction.** Given a selected card row and a line with one first failure, replace only that failed clause while retaining the row’s correct classification, scope, and permitted action.
