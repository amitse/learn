# 0060 — Audit One Action-Ready Verdict

**Date:** 2026-10-11  
**Lesson:** `0060-audit-one-action-ready-verdict.html`

## What was taught

- Audit a purported action-ready F2L Fresh E/F verdict in the fixed order: condition, evidence, then action.
- Stop at any drift in target pair, empty workspace type, retained cue (including timing), or checkpoint; the result is **FRESH EVIDENCE**, not an actionable drill decision.
- When conditions match, repair only the first unsupported clause: remove an invented cause from the evidence clause or reduce the conclusion to one evidence-matched action.

## Key non-obvious insights

- Accurate-looking mismatch wording cannot compensate for a condition failure; comparable attempts are the prerequisite for interpreting a verdict.
- “The grip is wrong” and similar explanations are stories unless the logs recorded them; the audit asks for literal first-mismatch evidence instead.
- Minimal repair preserves valid evidence and the existing controlled condition, so it is more useful than changing a pair, workspace, or algorithm.
