# 0010 — Finger Tricks

**Date:** 2026-06-28
**Lesson:** `0010-finger-tricks.html`

## What was taught

- **Standard grip**: finger-to-face assignments for R, U, F and their inverses in the context of the beginner method
- **R move**: right ring + pinky push the top edge of the right face forward
- **R′ move**: right thumb pulls the front edge of the right face toward you
- **U move**: right middle finger sweeps the top face left
- **U′ move**: left thumb sweeps the top face right — the key handoff between hands
- **U2 move**: two consecutive right-middle sweeps without releasing
- **F move**: right thumb pushes front face downward (clockwise)
- **F′ move**: right index finger lifts front face upward (counter-clockwise)
- Three sequenced practice steppers: R U R′ → R U R′ U′ → F R U R′ U′ F′
- Practice drill: 20 consecutive Right Triggers with a built-in stopwatch for pacing

## Key non-obvious insights

- The hardest transition in the Right Trigger is **R′ → U′**, not R → U. The left thumb must *arrive during* the R′ motion — not after a full stop.
- The F′ (right index up) and F (right thumb down) are different fingers, which surprises beginners who expect the same finger reversed.
- Letting go of the cube to reposition is the primary micro-habit that finger tricks eliminate. The goal is not speed but *continuity*.
- Drilling R U R′ (3-move fragment) as a single gesture before adding U′ is the most efficient learning path.
- Comfortable pace benchmark: 20 Right Triggers in 12–15 seconds (≈ 0.6–0.75s per trigger).
