# 0047 — Compare Fresh E/F After a Successor Drill

**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Lesson:** `0047-compare-fresh-e-and-f-after-a-successor-drill.html`

## What was taught

- After a fixed five-repetition successor run, Fresh E and Fresh F are two new attempts made under the same target pair, empty-workspace type, retained cue, and checkpoint.
- The comparison uses the first mismatch in each fresh attempt, rather than the run’s score or a general sense of fluency.
- Both fresh logs moving past the successor target supports retaining it; either log repeating the target calls for refinement or slower practice; a shared genuinely later mismatch supports a narrow successor drill; condition drift requires fresh comparable evidence.

## Key non-obvious insights

- Fresh E/F are not extra rows in the five-repetition run: they are the post-run test that permits a verdict.
- “Later” must mean later in the actual workflow order. A label that happens before the previous target cannot be treated as a later-stage bottleneck.
- One promising fresh attempt is insufficient: the shared result across two comparable logs prevents an accidental advance.
