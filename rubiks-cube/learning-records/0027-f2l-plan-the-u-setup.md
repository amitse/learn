# 0027 — F2L: Plan the U Setup

**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Lesson:** `0027-f2l-plan-the-u-setup.html`

## What was taught

- After reading a normalized F2L pair, use a deliberate single `U`-layer setup candidate (`0`, `U`, `U2`, or `U′`) rather than making exploratory turns.
- `U` turns change a top-layer piece’s position but not a corner’s sticker orientation or an edge’s orientation; a White-Up corner and an unready edge need normalization before a U setup scan.
- Each candidate must be followed by the two-part green-light check: known corner direction at the working side plus the matching edge at `UF` with its front sticker matching the front centre.
- A failed candidate is reversible: undo or re-read before trying another alignment; never compensate with a trigger through a protected F2L slot.

## Key non-obvious insights

- The four possible U alignments are a finite decision set, not a sequence to execute blindly. Naming one before turning cleanly separates planning from execution.
- A candidate can look promising because the corner reaches UFR/UFL yet still fail because the edge-centre check is wrong. The fixed centres remain the final objective reference.
- The correct result of a setup attempt may be “not this pair yet.” Preserving completed slots is more valuable than forcing a completion.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L: choose the working side from the corner orientation.** Given a ready corner on U, decide whether the right-facing or left-facing safe picture is the viable destination before rotating the whole cube and choosing a U setup candidate. This adds fast side selection while retaining the read → one setup → verify discipline.
