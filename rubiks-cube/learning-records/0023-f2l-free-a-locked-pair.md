# 0023 — F2L: Free a Locked Pair

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Lesson:** `0023-f2l-free-a-locked-pair.html`

## What was taught

- When a recognized F2L corner or matching edge is trapped in a wrong slot, `U` turns alone cannot make it available because they move only the top layer.
- First select an **empty working slot** with a whole-cube `y` rotation and protect all completed pairs.
- A short trigger at the empty working side can **free/eject** a misplaced piece to U: `R U R′` from front-right or `L′ U′ L` from front-left.
- After ejection, stop and inspect. Re-identify the pair, use `U`/`U′` to construct one of the known safe pictures, then insert with its trigger.

## Key non-obvious insights

- The same short trigger can insert a prepared pair or free a misplaced piece; its starting state determines its job.
- Ejection is a normalization step, not a sequence to repeat blindly. The mandatory inspection after it prevents compounding an orientation mistake.
- The safety rule remains unchanged: triggers operate only through an empty slot; `U` moves set up top-layer pieces while solved F2L pairs stay protected.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L: corner orientation on U.** Teach one common top-layer corner orientation that does not fit either safe picture immediately, and use an empty working slot to turn it into a recognizable right- or left-facing pairing picture. This extends recovery into deliberate corner control without a 41-case list.
