# 0034 — Choose the Next F2L Action After a Fresh Read

**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Lesson:** `0034-choose-the-next-f2l-action-after-a-fresh-read.html`

## What was taught

- A post-normalization observation is a fresh decision point: previous positional and readiness claims do not carry forward.
- After re-identifying the pair, its centre-defined slot, and the empty workspace, choose one of three actions: insert a fully green-lit pair; make one named U setup for a viable positional template; or normalize once through the empty slot and re-read.
- A viable positional template authorizes exactly one predicted `0`, `U`, `U2`, or `U′` setup—not an insertion trigger. The full green light must be checked again after the setup.
- A white-up corner or an unready edge with no viable position route is not fixed by cycling U turns. If no other pair is ready, normalize once and begin a new read.

## Key non-obvious insights

- Recovery success is not a promise that the original pair will be solved next; it produces evidence for the least-disruptive next action.
- A different fully ready pair has priority over continuing to manipulate an unready pair.
- “Insert,” “named setup,” and “normalize” are mutually exclusive next actions. Naming one prevents accidental trigger chaining.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L: run a complete read-to-insert decision loop.** Practise a concrete pair from fresh read through side selection, named setup (when viable), full green-light verification, and one authorized insertion—while explicitly stopping for recovery when authorization fails.
