# 0052 — Plan Matching Fresh E/F Evidence

**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Lesson:** `0052-plan-matching-fresh-e-f-evidence.html`

## What was taught

- When a repaired Fresh E/F verdict is evidence-needed, the next step is to write a matching collection plan before turning the cube.
- Fresh E and Fresh F must retain the same target pair, empty working-slot type, retained cue, and observation checkpoint.
- A failure under matching conditions remains useful evidence; an attempt with condition drift is replaced rather than compared or used to change the drill.

## Key non-obvious insights

- Copy the controlled condition, not a hoped-for outcome: comparability is the prerequisite for interpreting either success or failure.
- The retained cue begins before the attempt; using it late changes the practice condition even when the pair and workspace match.
- A replacement attempt repairs the evidence set without widening the target, changing an algorithm, or inventing a new explanation.
