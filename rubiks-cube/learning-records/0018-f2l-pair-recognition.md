# 0018 — F2L Pair Recognition

**Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Lesson:** `0018-f2l-pair-recognition.html`

## What was taught

- The visual recognition rule for an F2L pair: a white corner’s **two non-white colours** identify its matching edge.
- Those same two colours name the target slot, defined by the fixed side centres between them.
- A short verbal chain to make the recognition explicit before turning: **corner → matching edge → slot**.
- A stuck matching edge remains the correct partner; eject it first, then bring the pair to the U layer rather than forcing an insertion.

## Key non-obvious insights

- White is not part of the search key: it identifies the corner as an F2L corner, while the other two colours determine its partner.
- Piece location and orientation do not change pair identity. A green-red edge trapped in the middle layer is still the partner for the white-green-red corner.
- Centre colours, not the nearest empty location, determine a pair’s home slot.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L pairing setup:** take a recognized corner-edge pair on U, use U moves to align their relationship, then insert it with the appropriate right or left trigger. This builds directly on the recognition skill without adding a large case list.
