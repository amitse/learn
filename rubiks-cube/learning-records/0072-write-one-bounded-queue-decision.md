# 0072 — Write One Bounded Queue Decision

**Date:** 2026-11-05  
**Lesson:** `0072-write-one-bounded-queue-decision.html`

## What was taught

- A valid F2L checkpoint log may support a queue decision only by contributing its literal visible observation.
- The decision’s action is copied from the selected ACT source row; a valid log does not authorize a new drill, algorithm, or extra action.
- A bounded decision retains the source scope—target pair, empty-workspace type, cue/timing, and checkpoint—and gives exactly one next action.

## Key non-obvious insights

- “Successful,” “rushed,” and “bad grip” are conclusions or causes, not literal checkpoint observations; they cannot be smuggled into an evidence clause.
- Adding a follow-up insertion after an approved five-read drill crosses the one-action boundary even when the first action is correct.
- A precise decision can be useful without explaining why the cube state occurred; explanation is a separate, later question.
