# 0068 — Execute One F2L Queue Instruction

**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Lesson:** `0068-execute-one-f2l-queue-instruction.html`

## What was taught

- A repaired F2L queue instruction is executed as one row-local cube attempt: preserve its classification, target pair, empty-workspace type, retained cue, checkpoint, and one permitted action.
- An **ACT** row authorizes its approved drill; a **FRESH EVIDENCE** row authorizes one matching replacement attempt.
- The attempt ends at the stated checkpoint with a literal observation log, before any new verdict, cause, or follow-up action is added.

## Key non-obvious insights

- Changing the pair, workspace, cue timing, or checkpoint mid-attempt destroys the comparison condition even if the turns were useful practice.
- A checkpoint log records what is visible, not why it happened or whether the drill succeeded.
- Stopping at the checkpoint keeps one attempt attributable to one instruction, which is essential for recoverable F2L practice.
