# 0019 — F2L Working Slot

**Date:** 2026-07-21
**Lesson:** `0019-f2l-working-slot.html`

## What was taught

- The **working slot**: deliberately place one unsolved F2L target slot at front-right before preparing its pair.
- The corner’s two non-white colours and the fixed side centres determine the target; a whole-cube `y` rotation determines which slot is front-right.
- A repeatable planning loop: name the pair → place its empty slot front-right → free trapped pieces to U → use U turns for setup → insert through the working slot.
- Solved F2L pairs should be protected: U turns rearrange the top layer without disturbing solved bottom two-layer slots; do not run a trigger through a completed slot.

## Key non-obvious insights

- “Front-right” is not a permanent physical location; it is a deliberate viewpoint selected with a whole-cube rotation after the target has been identified.
- The empty slot is a safety boundary. If an intended trigger would manipulate a solved slot, reset the cube orientation rather than repairing damage afterward.
- This adds control, not a new case list: it lets Amit apply the existing F2L triggers with a clear workspace.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L pair formation:** with both identified pieces on U and the target slot front-right, learn one common corner-edge relationship and use setup U moves to join them before insertion. This is the next small step toward intuitive F2L without memorising all 41 cases.
