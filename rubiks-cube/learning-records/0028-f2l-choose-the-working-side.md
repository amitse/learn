# 0028 — F2L: Choose the Working Side

**Date:** 2026-08-08  
**Lesson:** `0028-f2l-choose-the-working-side.html`

## What was taught

- A ready F2L corner’s white-sticker direction selects the viable working side: white Right → front-right; white Left → front-left.
- A whole-cube `y` rotation should bring the chosen empty target slot to the front before any U-layer setup candidate is considered.
- Side selection does not authorize insertion: the learner must still find the corner at UFR/UFL, verify the matching edge at UF against the front centre, and use the matching trigger only after the two-part green light.
- A White-Up corner has no selected side; it requires normalization through an empty working slot and a fresh read.

## Key non-obvious insights

- `y` and `U` have distinct jobs: `y` changes the solver’s viewpoint/workspace while preserving the cube state, whereas `U` tests top-layer alignment within the selected workspace.
- Choosing the side from the corner before scanning U prevents the common error of trying the right trigger merely because front-right happens to be convenient.
- The target slot is defined by fixed centres and the pair’s non-white colours—not by whichever front face is currently held.

## Zone of proximal development — next

**F2L: decide whether a U setup candidate is worth trying.** Given a selected right or left working side, use the corner’s current U-layer position and the matching edge’s position to eliminate obviously impossible U candidates before turning, while keeping the centre-based green-light check.
