# 0040 — Select a Testable F2L Micro-Drill

**Date:** 2026-09-01  
**Lesson:** `0040-select-a-testable-f2l-micro-drill.html`

## What was taught

- A testable F2L micro-drill preserves a comparable condition, isolates the current earliest workflow stage, names an observable checkpoint, and finishes with a fresh comparable log.
- Given two candidate drills, select the one that targets the first mismatch rather than mixing planning, green-light recognition, trigger execution, and timing.
- A vague but relevant drill can be repaired by adding its missing checkpoint, without widening its target or adding extra repetitions.

## Key non-obvious insights

- Repetition quantity does not make a practice plan diagnostic; the checkpoint is what makes the outcome interpretable.
- A planning failure must be observed at destination positions, a green-light failure before the trigger, and an execution failure immediately after an authorized trigger.
- Isolating one pair and one empty workspace makes F2L recovery evidence-based and protects already solved pairs.
