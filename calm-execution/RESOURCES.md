# Calm Execution Resources

## Knowledge

- [Gardner, Lally & Wardle — Making health habitual (2012)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3505409/)
  Accessible review of habit formation. Useful for the core idea that repeated behaviour in a stable context can become cue-driven and less dependent on momentary motivation. The article explicitly distinguishes automatic cue-triggered habits from merely frequent behaviour.

- User-supplied notes attributed to Derek Sivers, *Hell Yeah or No* (captured 2026-07-19)
  Use as a source of prompts and operating principles: choose fewer commitments, make goals change today’s behaviour, protect focus, and favour output over more consumption. Treat the supplied distillation as prompts, not a verified representation of the book.

## Practice

- Daily start log
  A one-line record: date, chosen action, cue, smallest first step, started? This is the evidence base for the course.

## Wisdom

- A trusted colleague or accountability partner
  Use only for reviewing the design of the system when it repeatedly fails—not for outsourcing responsibility or adding shame.
