# Learning Record 0062 — Audit a Classified-Pair Instruction

## Date
2026-09-20

## Lesson
0062 — Audit a Classified-Pair Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner compares the exact Lesson 61 instruction with each of its two covered ordinary attempts before interpreting outcomes.
- Each attempt receives one evidence label: `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`; missing details are not reconstructed from memory.
- If either label is `not followed` or `unclear`, the learner repeats the exact instruction for two ordinary attempts without adding a change or reading cue/start outcomes.
- Only two `followed` labels create a verified pair ready for the existing cue-then-start classification.

## Key insight

A pair can answer a plan only after the plan was enacted: **audit the exact sentence first; if either attempt is unverified, repeat it calmly before reading outcomes.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can now turn a classified pair into a precise next instruction, but whether that instruction was enacted is still separate from whether it worked. This fidelity gate preserves the observable cue, exact two-minute action, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add duration, catch-up work, multiple changes, or more tracking.

## What comes next

After both covered attempts are labelled `followed`, teach a compact classified-pair outcome reading: read `cue occurred?` then `two-minute action started?`, label each `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`, and retain unless the same readable earliest break appears twice and supports one already-specified matching test.
