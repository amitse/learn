# Learning Record 0107 — Audit One Classified-Pair Instruction

## Date
2026-11-04

## Lesson
0107 — Audit One Classified-Pair Instruction

## What was taught

- Compare the exact Lesson 106 instruction with each of its two covered ordinary attempts.
- Label each attempt `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`.
- Read cue and start outcomes only when both attempts are `followed`.
- When either label is `not followed` or `unclear`, repeat the exact instruction for two ordinary attempts without adding a change.

## Key insight

The first question about a planned adjustment is whether it was enacted. An unclear or non-followed pair calls for readable evidence, not an outcome interpretation or redesign.

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can turn a classified pair into one bounded instruction naming a cue, exact two-minute action, and two-attempt boundary. This lesson adds only an enactment gate before outcome classification. Preserve the one-variable limit and no-penalty stance; do not add a duration target, output target, reminder, second test, catch-up work, or character explanation.

## What comes next

After an audit-passed pair, classify each attempt cue-first: read `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`; label `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear` before deciding whether to retain/gather or use one already-specified matching bounded test.
