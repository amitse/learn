## Date
2026-11-10

## Lesson
0113 — Audit One Evidence-Bound Instruction

## What was taught

- Before interpreting two attempts covered by the Lesson 112 instruction, compare each attempt with that exact sentence.
- Label each attempt `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear` according to whether the stated cue, exact two-minute action, and any active test were used.
- If either label is `not followed` or `unclear`, repeat the instruction unchanged for two ordinary attempts; do not classify cue or start outcomes yet.
- Only an audit-passed pair (`followed`, `followed`) proceeds to cue-first outcome classification.

## Key insight

**Enactment is evidence too.** A result cannot speak for an instruction that was not clearly used.

## Zone of proximal development notes

This lesson adds one gate before reading outcomes: verify enactment of that sentence across the two covered attempts. Preserve the existing observable cue, exact two-minute action, two-attempt boundary, one-variable limit, and no-penalty stance. Do not add a duration target, output target, reminder, catch-up work, second test, or character explanation.
