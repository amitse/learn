# Learning Record 0002 — Friction Removal

## Date
2026-07-22

## Lesson
0002 — Remove One Barrier Before the Cue

## What was taught

- The start ritual now has a runway: cue → prepared first object → two-minute action.
- A barrier may be physical, digital, or an unresolved first decision.
- The learner practices identifying the earliest barrier and removing exactly one before the cue.
- The daily log gains two inspectable fields: `setup done?` and `started?`.

## Key insight

Preparation is not a productivity project. It is one small, observable move that makes the already-chosen first action available at the cue.

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a concrete cue and two-minute action from Lesson 1 but has not yet demonstrated consistency. Keep the system light: one barrier only, no expectation of longer work, and treat any miss as data about the remaining barrier.

## What comes next

Lesson 0003 should teach a neutral miss review: separate the cue, setup, and first action so a missed start produces one specific system adjustment rather than guilt.
