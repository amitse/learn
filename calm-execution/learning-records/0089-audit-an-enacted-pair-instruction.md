# Learning Record 0089 — Audit an Enacted-Pair Instruction

## Date
2026-10-17

## Lesson
0089 — Audit an Enacted-Pair Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner compares the exact Lesson 88 instruction with each of its two covered ordinary attempts before reading outcomes.
- Each attempt receives one enactment label: `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`.
- If either label is `not followed` or `unclear`, the learner repeats the exact instruction for two ordinary attempts without adding a change or interpreting cue/start outcomes.
- Only two `followed` labels create an enacted pair ready for cue-first outcome classification.

## Key insight

Before outcome data can guide the ritual, establish whether the written ritual was enacted: **audit the exact sentence first; repeat it unchanged until both attempts are readable as followed.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can translate a cue-first classified pair into an exact two-attempt instruction. This lesson adds the immediate fidelity gate while preserving the observable cue, exact two-minute action, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add duration, catch-up work, a second test, or an explanation of character.

## What comes next

When both covered attempts are `followed`, classify each outcome in order: `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`; use `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`, and retain/gather records unless the same readable earliest break appears twice and has one already-specified matching test.
