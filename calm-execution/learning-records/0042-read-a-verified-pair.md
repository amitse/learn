## What was taught

- After the Lesson 41 audit confirms both attempts followed the instruction, the learner reads each record in order: `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`.
- Each attempt receives one earliest readable label: `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`.
- An unclear field directs the learner to retain the current instruction and gather two readable ordinary attempts rather than reconstructing an outcome.
- Only the same `cue` or `start-side` label twice supports one already-specified matching test; all other verified pairs retain the plan for two ordinary attempts.

## Key insight

Verification and triage are separate moves: **after confirming the plan was followed, read cue before start and change only when the same earliest break appears twice.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

This adds the immediate, bounded interpretation step without increasing duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, no-penalty stance, and one-variable approach. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.
