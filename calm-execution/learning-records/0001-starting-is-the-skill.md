# Learning Record 0001 — Starting Is the Skill

## Date
2026-07-21

## Lesson
0001 — Design the First Two Minutes

## What was taught

- A vague task cannot be started reliably; the first action must be observable and physical.
- A useful plan has three parts: a cue, a concrete action, and a deliberately small duration.
- The goal is to create evidence of starting, not to force a long work session.
- Recording the start makes the system inspectable when it fails.

## Key insight

The target is not “be motivated.” The target is: **when a known cue occurs, begin one specified action for two minutes.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

This is the first lesson. Do not assume a stable routine or a preferred work time. Future lessons should use the start log to identify usable cues and recurring friction.

## What comes next

Lesson 0002 should teach friction removal: prepare the environment so the chosen first action is easier than avoidance.
