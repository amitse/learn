# Learning Record 0050 — Audit a Re-audited Pair Instruction

## Date
2026-09-08

## Lesson
0050 — Audit a Re-audited Pair Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner compares the exact Lesson 49 instruction with each of its two covered ordinary attempts.
- Each attempt receives one enactment label: `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`.
- If either attempt is `not followed` or `unclear`, the learner repeats the same instruction for two ordinary attempts without interpreting cue/start results or adding a change.
- Only two `followed` labels make the pair ready for the existing cue/start-side classification.

## Key insight

Interpretation comes after enactment is readable: **audit the sentence first; when either attempt is not followed or unclear, repeat it unchanged before reading outcomes.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can write a re-audited-pair instruction and has previously practised the same audit structure. This refresh applies that structure to the newly written instruction without increasing duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.

## What comes next

After both covered attempts are labelled `followed`, teach a compact re-audited-pair classification: label each readable outcome `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`, then retain/gather unless the same readable break appears twice and supports one already-specified matching test.
