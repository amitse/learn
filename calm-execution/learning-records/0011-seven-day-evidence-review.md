# Learning Record 0011 — Seven-Day Evidence Review

## Date

2026-07-31

## Lesson

0011 — Run a Seven-Day Evidence Review

## What was taught

- The learner counts intentional starts in the last seven records; a primary- or fallback-cue start counts, including a start followed by stopping at the finish line.
- The count is progress evidence toward the 20-in-30-day mission, not a streak, verdict, or demand for catch-up work.
- The learner reads the existing record in earliest-link order: cue, setup, then action.
- The review selects one small next experiment, or deliberately selects no change when the pattern is unclear.

## Key insight

A weekly count can remain calm when it directs one system test rather than self-evaluation: **count starts, inspect the earliest recurring link, and change one thing—or nothing—next.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a bounded start ritual with a primary cue, one fallback, one detour protection, a finish line, and an optional continuation, but consistency is not established. This introduces mission-level evidence without adding a daily score, raising duration, or requiring output. Preserve the one-attempt rhythm and keep a low count from becoming pressure.

## What comes next

Lesson 0012 should teach a simple reset after a disrupted week: restart with the same smallest ritual and one chosen adjustment, rather than compensating for missed days or redesigning the whole system.
