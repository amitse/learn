# Learning Record 0008 — Block One Digital Detour

## Date

2026-07-28

## Lesson

0008 — Block One Digital Detour

## What was taught

- The learner protects the existing cue-based start by making one predictable digital detour temporarily unavailable.
- The intervention is deliberately narrow and reversible: one detour, one block, only through the two-minute opening action.
- The primary cue, fallback rule, setup, and two-minute action remain unchanged.
- The daily record gains two inspectable fields: `detour blocked?` and `started?`.

## Key insight

The aim is not to eliminate distraction. It is to create a clear doorway: **at the cue, block one predictable detour briefly, then do the same two-minute action.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a primary cue, a prepared first action, a bounded fallback, and a light record, but consistency is not established. This lesson introduces one environmental adjustment without adding duration, output goals, a broader digital detox, or a second recovery plan. Keep all prior components stable so the result is interpretable.

## What comes next

Lesson 0009 should teach how to make the opening action visibly complete: define a two-minute finish line so beginning does not turn into deciding when the first tiny step is done.
