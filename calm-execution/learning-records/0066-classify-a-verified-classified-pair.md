## What was taught

- Only after both covered attempts are labelled `followed` does the learner read outcomes.
- The learner reads `cue occurred?` then `two-minute action started?` and assigns each attempt `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`.
- A missing fact stays `unclear`; it is not reconstructed from memory.
- The learner retains the existing plan and gathers two ordinary readable records unless the same readable `cue` or `start-side` break appears twice and supports one already-specified matching test.

## Key insight

A verified instruction makes a small pair readable: **cue first, start second, then retain the small plan unless the same readable earliest break appears twice.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

This lesson supplies the immediate outcome-reading step while preserving the observable cue, exact two-minute action, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add duration, catch-up work, multiple changes, or more tracking.
