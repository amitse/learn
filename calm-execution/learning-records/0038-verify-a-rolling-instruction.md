## What was taught

- The learner compares the last written rolling instruction with the two ordinary attempts it covered before interpreting their outcome.
- The learner confirms whether the instruction to retain, gather records, or use one exact bounded test was followed in both attempts.
- An instruction not followed or not verifiable is labelled `incomplete`; the learner repeats the same instruction and gathers two readable records rather than explaining the result.
- Only after verification does the learner use the familiar two-record boundary: retain when mixed or workable, or run one already-specified matching test when the same earliest break appears twice.

## Key insight

A record can answer a plan only when the plan was enacted: **verify the rolling instruction first; then read cue and start, and make one small next instruction.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a carry-forward ritual and a rolling two-record review, while actual evidence remains unknown. This lesson adds a fidelity check to that existing loop without increasing duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable approach. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.
