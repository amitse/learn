## Lesson

0013 — Change One Link

## What was taught

- The learner distinguishes a one-link system adjustment from a multi-part redesign.
- The learner keeps the existing primary cue, two-minute action, and finish line stable while naming one test, or deliberately names no change.
- The next attempt remains one ordinary attempt with the existing evidence fields.
- The learner uses the resulting record before deciding whether to make another adjustment.

## Key insight

A useful experiment preserves a readable before-and-after: **hold the ritual steady, test one named link once, then read the record.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a complete bounded ritual, a neutral review process, a fallback, and an evidence review. Consistency remains unproven. This lesson prevents the accumulated system from becoming an ever-changing project. Do not add duration, extra reminders, output goals, catch-up work, or multiple adjustments.
