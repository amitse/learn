## What was taught

- The cue, setup, fallback rule, and temporary detour block remain unchanged.
- The learner tests one finish line during a single real attempt and records `finish line reached? y/n` alongside `started? y/n`.

## Key insight

Beginning need not become an open-ended negotiation: **at the cue, do the prepared action until one visible condition is true, then stop or freely continue.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a deliberately bounded start ritual with a primary cue, one fallback, light evidence, and one narrow digital protection. Consistency is still not established. This lesson resolves ambiguity inside the two-minute action without increasing duration, output, reminders, or measurement burden. Do not add a second finish line or treat reaching it as a productivity score.

## What comes next
