# Learning Record 0114 — Classify One Audit-Passed Pair

## Date
2026-11-11

## Lesson
0114 — Classify One Audit-Passed Pair

## What was taught

- Only after both covered attempts are labelled `followed` in the enactment audit, classify each attempt cue-first.
- Use exactly one label per attempt: `cue` when the cue did not occur or was not noticed; `start-side` when the cue occurred but the exact two-minute action did not start; `none` when both occurred; or `unclear` when the record cannot tell.
- A mixed, working, or unclear pair calls for retaining the plan and gathering two ordinary records.
- Only the same readable `cue` or `start-side` break twice can support one already-specified matching bounded test.

## Key insight

**Outcome reading comes after enactment verification.** A pair identifies one link to inspect; it does not license a full redesign or a verdict about the learner.

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can audit whether an evidence-bound instruction was followed across two attempts. This lesson adds the smallest next distinction: cue-first classification of an audit-passed pair. Preserve the observable cue, exact two-minute action, two-attempt boundary, one-variable limit, and no-penalty stance. Do not add a duration target, output target, reminder, catch-up work, second test, or character explanation.

## What comes next

Turn the classified audit-passed pair into one evidence-bound next instruction: retain and gather for mixed, working, or unclear evidence; use only one already-specified matching test when the same readable break appears twice.
