## What was taught

## Key insight

A classification becomes a calm next step only when it is checkable: **write one next move, name the stable cue and exact two-minute action, use it twice, then audit enactment before interpreting outcomes.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can audit an instruction and classify a re-audited pair, while actual records remain unknown. This lesson restores the immediate output of classification without raising duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.

## What comes next

After the instruction covers two ordinary attempts, teach a compact audit: compare the exact sentence with both attempts, label each `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`, and repeat the same instruction whenever enactment is not yet readable.
