## What was taught

- The learner compares the exact Lesson 70 instruction with each of its two covered ordinary attempts.
- Each attempt receives one enactment label: `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`.
- If either label is `not followed` or `unclear`, the learner repeats the exact instruction for two ordinary attempts before interpreting cue or start outcomes.
- Only two `followed` labels permit the next cue-then-start outcome reading.

## Key insight

Before a result can inform a plan, establish whether the plan was enacted: **repeat the same instruction until two attempts are readable as followed.**
