## Lesson
0065 — Audit a Verified Classified-Pair Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner compares the exact Lesson 64 instruction with each of its two covered ordinary attempts before interpreting outcomes.
- Each attempt receives one enactment label: `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`; missing details are not reconstructed from memory.
- If either label is `not followed` or `unclear`, the learner repeats the exact instruction for two ordinary attempts without a new change or cue/start interpretation.
- Only two `followed` labels create a verified pair ready for the existing cue-then-start classification.

## Key insight

Interpretation comes after enactment is readable: **audit the exact sentence first; when either attempt is not followed or unclear, repeat it unchanged before reading outcomes.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can turn a verified classified pair into a precise two-attempt instruction, but whether it was enacted is separate from whether it worked. This fidelity gate preserves the observable cue, exact two-minute action, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add duration, catch-up work, multiple changes, or more tracking.

## What comes next

After both covered attempts are labelled `followed`, teach a compact verified classified-pair outcome reading: read `cue occurred?` then `two-minute action started?`, label each `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`, and retain unless the same readable earliest break appears twice and supports one already-specified matching test.
