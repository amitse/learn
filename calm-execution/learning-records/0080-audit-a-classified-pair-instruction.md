# Learning Record 0080 — Audit a Classified-Pair Instruction

## Date
2026-10-08

## Lesson
0080 — Audit a Classified-Pair Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner compares each of two covered attempts with the exact Lesson 79 instruction, rather than with intention or outcome.
- Each attempt receives one enactment label: `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`.
- Only two `followed` labels permit a cue-first outcome reading; otherwise the identical instruction is repeated for two ordinary attempts.

## Key insight

Before evidence can say anything about a classified-pair instruction, the evidence must show whether the instruction was actually used: **cue, exact two-minute action, and next move**.

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can translate a classified, audit-passed pair into a bounded instruction. This lesson adds only an enactment check; it does not change duration, frequency, cue, action, or active test. Maintain the no-penalty stance: unmatched or unclear records call for repeating the same plan and gathering readable evidence, not redesign, catch-up work, or a conclusion about ability.

## What comes next

When both covered attempts are `followed`, classify their outcomes cue-first: `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`. Retain and gather records unless the same readable earliest break appears twice and supports one already-specified matching test.
