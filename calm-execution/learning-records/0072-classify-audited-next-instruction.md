# Learning Record 0072 — Classify an Audited Next Instruction

## Date
2026-09-30

## Lesson
0072 — Classify an Audited Next Instruction

## What was taught

- After the Lesson 71 audit shows two `followed` attempts, the learner reads each attempt in order: `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`.
- The learner labels each outcome `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`.
- Only the same readable `cue` or `start-side` break twice, with one already-specified matching test, supports that one test. Otherwise the learner retains the instruction and gathers two ordinary records.

## Key insight

An outcome is interpretable only after enactment is verified: **read cue before start, and change only at a repeated, readable break.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can audit whether an exact next instruction was enacted across two attempts. This lesson adds outcome classification without changing the observable cue, exact two-minute action, chosen frequency, active test, or no-penalty stance. Do not add duration, tracking fields, output targets, a second test, or catch-up work.

## What comes next

Turn a classified verified pair into one exact next instruction: retain and gather two ordinary attempts for mixed, workable, or incomplete evidence; or run the single already-specified matching test when the same readable break appears twice.
