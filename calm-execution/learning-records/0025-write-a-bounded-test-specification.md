## What was taught

- The learner turns a triage-supported refinement into one readable sentence: link, exact bounded change, and stable remainder.
- No repeated earliest break means no test: retain the current plan for one ordinary attempt.
- A repeated cue break permits one cue test; a repeated start-side break permits one setup *or* opening-action test.
- The learner makes one ordinary attempt before interpreting the result or changing another link.

## Key insight

A test stays interpretable when it says what changes and what does not: **test one named link once; keep the rest of the small plan stable.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a carry-forward ritual, a four-to-six-record triage, and a stability boundary, but actual evidence remains unknown. This lesson translates a supported decision into a concrete instruction without increasing duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable approach. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger system.

## What comes next
