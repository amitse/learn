## Date

## Lesson
0087 — Classify an Enacted Cue-First Pair

## What was taught

- Only after both covered attempts are marked `followed`, the learner reads each in order: `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`.
- Each attempt receives one label: `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`.
- Two matching readable `cue` or `start-side` labels may carry forward one already-specified matching bounded test.
- Mixed, workable, or incomplete pairs retain the current cue and exact two-minute action and gather two ordinary readable records.

## Key insight

A result becomes useful without becoming personal when it is read in observable order: **cue first, start second; retain unless the same readable earliest break appears twice.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can write and audit a two-attempt cue-first instruction. This lesson adds only outcome classification after enactment has been verified. Preserve the two-minute baseline, observable cue, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add duration, catch-up work, a second test, or a character explanation.

## What comes next

Turn the classified enacted pair into one exact next instruction: retain and gather for two ordinary attempts, or use one already-specified matching test when the same readable `cue` or `start-side` break appears twice. Audit enactment before interpreting the next outcomes.
