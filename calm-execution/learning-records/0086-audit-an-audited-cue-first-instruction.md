## Date

## Lesson
0086 — Audit an Audited Cue-First Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner compares the exact Lesson 85 sentence with each of its two covered ordinary attempts before reading outcomes.
- Each attempt receives one enactment label: `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`; missing facts are not reconstructed from memory.
- If either label is `not followed` or `unclear`, the learner repeats the exact instruction for two ordinary attempts without adding a change or interpreting cue/start outcomes.
- Only two `followed` labels create a verified pair ready for cue-first outcome classification.

## Key insight

An outcome can inform the plan only after the plan was enacted: **audit the exact sentence first; repeat it unchanged until both covered attempts are readable as followed.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can turn a classified audited pair into an exact two-attempt instruction. This lesson adds the immediate fidelity gate, preserving the observable cue, exact two-minute action, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add duration, catch-up work, a second test, or an explanation of character.

## What comes next

When both covered attempts are `followed`, teach cue-first outcome classification: label each attempt `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`; retain and gather records unless the same readable earliest break appears twice and supports one already-specified matching test.
