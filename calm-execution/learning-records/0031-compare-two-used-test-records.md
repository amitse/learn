# Learning Record 0031 — Compare Two Used Test Records

## Date
2026-08-20

## Lesson
0031 — Compare Two Used Test Records

## What was taught

- The learner compares two ordinary attempts after a bounded test in a fixed order: `test used?`, `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`.
- A record with an unused or unclear test does not answer the test question and directs the learner to gather ordinary evidence without adding a change.
- Two used records without clear added friction support retaining the same test for two more ordinary attempts.
- Clear added friction supports returning to the prior plan; mixed results support gathering two ordinary records rather than forcing an explanation.

## Key insight

Two records support a modest instruction, not a verdict: **confirm the test was used twice, then retain it, return for clear friction, or gather ordinary evidence without changing another link.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a carry-forward ritual, stability boundary, test specification, fidelity check, and one-record refresh, while actual evidence remains unknown. This lesson introduces a two-record comparison without increasing duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable approach. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.

## What comes next

After two more ordinary attempts, teach a compact four-record retention check: confirm the same test was used, read cue and start, and retain the plan unless clear added friction or the same earliest break appears twice.
