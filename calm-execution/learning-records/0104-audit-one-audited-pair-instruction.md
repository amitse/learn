## Lesson

## What was taught

- Put the exact Lesson 103 instruction beside both covered ordinary attempts before looking at cue or start outcomes.
- Audit each attempt against the one move, observable cue, exact two-minute action, and two-attempt boundary.
- Label each attempt `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`.
- When either label is not `followed`, repeat the exact instruction unchanged for two ordinary attempts; only two `followed` labels permit cue-first outcome classification.

## Key insight

The plan must be readable before its result is interpreted: **audit enactment first; read outcomes second.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

This lesson adds one fidelity gate: check whether that exact instruction was enacted before drawing an outcome conclusion. Preserve the observable cue, exact two-minute action, two-attempt boundary, one-variable limit, and no-penalty stance. Do not add a duration goal, output goal, reminder, second test, catch-up work, or character explanation.
