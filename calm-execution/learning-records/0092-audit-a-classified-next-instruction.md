## What was taught

- The learner compares the exact Lesson 91 sentence with each of its two covered ordinary attempts before reading outcomes.
- Each attempt receives one enactment label: `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`.
- Only two `followed` labels make the pair ready for cue-first outcome classification.
- If either attempt is `not followed` or `unclear`, the learner repeats the exact instruction for two ordinary attempts without redesign, catch-up work, or outcome interpretation.

## Key insight

A result can only inform the plan after the plan was enacted: **audit the exact sentence first; repeat it unchanged until both covered attempts are readable as followed.**

Do not add duration, a second test, extra tracking, output targets, or a character explanation.
