# Learning Record 0051 — Classify a Re-audited Pair

## Date
2026-09-09

## Lesson
0051 — Classify a Re-audited Pair

## What was taught

- After both attempts pass the Lesson 50 enactment audit, the learner reads each record in order: `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`.
- Each readable attempt receives one label: `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`.
- Working, mixed, or unclear pairs direct the learner to retain the current instruction and gather two ordinary readable records.
- Only two matching readable `cue` or `start-side` labels support one already-specified matching bounded test; no other link changes.

## Key insight

A re-audited pair is small enough to read without daily redesign: **cue first, start second, then retain unless the same readable earliest break appears twice.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can write and audit a re-audited-pair instruction, but actual records remain unknown. This lesson adds the immediate outcome-classification step to that established loop without increasing duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.

## What comes next

After the pair is classified, teach a compact next-instruction writing drill: write a retain/gather instruction for two ordinary attempts, or name only one already-specified matching test, with the stable cue and exact two-minute action included.
