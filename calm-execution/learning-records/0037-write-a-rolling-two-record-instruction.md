## What was taught

- The learner reads the latest two ordinary records in a fixed order: `test used?` (when applicable), `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`.
- The learner labels each record `incomplete`, `cue`, `start-side`, or `none` without reconstructing unclear fields from memory.
- Incomplete, mixed, or workable records produce one rolling instruction: retain the current plan for two ordinary attempts and review the newest two records.
- The same earliest break in both readable records supports exactly one already-specified matching bounded test; no other link changes.

## Key insight

A rolling review prevents daily redesign: **read the latest two records, write one small instruction, and let the next two attempts be ordinary.**

Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable approach. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger system.
