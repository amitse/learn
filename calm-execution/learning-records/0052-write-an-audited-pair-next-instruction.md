## Date

## Lesson
0052 — Write an Audited-Pair Next Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner converts the Lesson 51 classified, audited pair into one exact instruction for the next two ordinary attempts.
- Mixed, working, or unclear classifications direct the learner to retain the existing plan and gather two readable records.
- Two matching readable `cue` labels support only one already-specified cue test; two `start-side` labels support only one already-specified setup or opening-action test.
- The instruction names the stable cue and exact two-minute action, making its enactment auditable next.

## Key insight

A classification becomes calm action when it produces one checkable sentence: **name one supported move, keep the cue and two-minute action stable, use it twice, then audit enactment.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can audit an instruction and classify a re-audited pair, while actual records remain unknown. This lesson supplies the immediate operational output of that classification without increasing duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.

## What comes next

After the instruction covers two ordinary attempts, teach a compact enactment audit: compare the exact sentence with both attempts, label each `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`, and repeat the instruction unchanged whenever enactment is not yet readable.
