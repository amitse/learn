# Learning Record 0043 — Write a Verified Next Instruction

## Date
2026-09-01

## Lesson
0043 — Write a Verified Next Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner converts a verified-pair result into one exact next instruction.
- An unclear pair directs the learner to gather two readable ordinary attempts with the current plan.
- A repeated `cue` or `start-side` label directs one already-specified matching test; a mixed or workable pair directs retention for two ordinary attempts.
- Every instruction names the stable cue and exact two-minute action, so a later audit has a clear object to compare.

## Key insight

A review becomes usable only when its result can be enacted and checked: **write one next move, state the stable cue and two-minute action, then use it for two ordinary attempts.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has audited a two-attempt instruction and read a verified pair, while actual evidence remains unknown. This adds the immediate output of that reading without increasing duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, no-penalty stance, and one-variable approach. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.

## What comes next

After the instruction covers two ordinary attempts, teach an audit that checks the exact resulting sentence against both attempts before interpreting cue and start outcomes.
