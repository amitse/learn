## What was taught

- After both covered attempts pass the Lesson 47 instruction audit, the learner reads each record in order: `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`.
- Each attempt receives one earliest readable label: `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`.
- An unclear field directs the learner to retain the current instruction and gather two readable ordinary attempts rather than reconstructing an outcome.
- Only the same readable `cue` or `start-side` break twice supports one already-specified matching bounded test; otherwise the learner retains the plan for two ordinary attempts.

## Key insight

A re-audited pair can be read without daily redesign: **cue first, start second, then retain unless the same readable break appears twice.**

Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.
