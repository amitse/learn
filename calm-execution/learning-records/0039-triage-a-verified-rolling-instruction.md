# Learning Record 0039 — Triage a Verified Rolling Instruction

## Date
2026-08-28

## Lesson
0039 — Triage a Verified Rolling Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner first verifies that the prior rolling instruction was followed in both covered attempts.
- For a verified pair, the learner labels each record at its earliest readable break: `none`, `cue`, or `start-side`.
- Incomplete verification or unclear fields direct the learner to repeat or retain the current instruction and gather two readable records.
- Only `cue` twice supports one already-specified cue test; only `start-side` twice supports one already-specified setup or opening-action test. All other pairs retain the plan for two ordinary attempts.

## Key insight

A two-record review stays useful when it has a fixed order: **verify the instruction, label the earliest break, then write one small next instruction.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a carry-forward ritual, rolling instruction, and fidelity check, while actual evidence remains unknown. This lesson joins fidelity and triage into one short decision loop without claiming that two records prove a cause. Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable approach. Do not add duration, catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger tracking system.

## What comes next

After the next two ordinary attempts, teach a compact instruction-writing practice that turns a verified two-record triage result into one exact sentence with the stable parts named, so the next fidelity check has a clear object to compare.
