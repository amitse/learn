## Lesson

0004 — Anchor One Reliable Cue

## What was taught

- The existing start ritual now begins with one observable event rather than a broad time window or a feeling.
- A useful cue is dependable, noticeable, and close to the prepared first action.
- The learner writes one testable cue sentence: `After [event], I will [two-minute action].`
- The daily log preserves the existing fields: `setup done?` and `started?`.

## Key insight

The cue does not have to create motivation. Its job is to make the next move unambiguous: **after one concrete event, begin the already-prepared two-minute action.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a concrete first action, a one-barrier setup, and a neutral review, but has not established a reliable trigger. Keep this to one anchor and one trial per day. Do not introduce streak targets, multiple reminders, catch-up work, or longer work expectations.

## What comes next

Lesson 0005 should teach a minimal visible start record: record the cue, setup, and yes/no start immediately after the attempt so the system has evidence without becoming burdensome.
