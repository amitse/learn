# Learning Record 0003 — Neutral Miss Review

## Date
2026-07-23

## Lesson
0003 — Review a Miss Neutrally

## What was taught

- A missed start can be reviewed at one of three inspectable points: cue, setup, or first action.
- The learner identifies the earliest break rather than explaining the miss as a personal flaw.
- The review ends with one small, testable adjustment for tomorrow.
- The start log gains a brief miss field: `missed because: cue/setup/action` and `tomorrow’s one change`.

## Key insight

A miss is useful only when it produces a specific next experiment: **which part broke first, and what one small change will I test tomorrow?**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has designed a cue, two-minute action, and one-barrier setup, but consistency is not yet established. Keep reviews brief and neutral; do not add scoring, catch-up work, or demands for longer sessions.

## What comes next

Lesson 0004 should teach choosing one reliable daily cue—anchoring the existing two-minute start to a concrete, repeatable event without introducing extra goals.
