# Learning Record 0010 — Choose Your Post-Start Step

## Date

2026-07-30

## Lesson

0010 — Choose Your Post-Start Step

## What was taught

- After the established two-minute finish line, the learner deliberately chooses either to stop or to do one pre-defined five-minute continuation.
- Stopping is a valid completion of the start ritual, not a miss.
- The continuation is named in observable terms before the cue; it is not an open-ended instruction to keep working.
- The record gains one light choice field: `chose stop/continue`, alongside `finish line reached?` and `started?`.

## Key insight

A reliable beginning does not create a debt to keep working: **after the visible finish line, deliberately stop or choose one pre-defined five-minute next step.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a bounded ritual with a concrete cue, setup, fallback, detour protection, and finish line, but consistency is still not established. This adds a single choice immediately after the start without raising the baseline duration. Keep stopping legitimate; avoid targets for continuations, session length, output, or catch-up work.

## What comes next
