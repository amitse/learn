# Learning Record 0035 — Write a Maintenance Handoff

## Date
2026-08-24

## Lesson
0035 — Write a Maintenance Handoff

## What was taught

- The learner turns the six-record continuity decision into one written instruction for the next two ordinary attempts.
- When no earliest break repeats twice, the handoff retains the same cue, two-minute action, chosen frequency, and any current test, then schedules a two-record review.
- Incomplete evidence directs the learner to keep the current instruction and gather two ordinary records; a repeated break directs one already-specified matching bounded test on the next ordinary attempt.
- The interactive card provides immediate feedback on whether the handoff has a concrete instruction, the appropriate decision branch, and a no-expansion boundary.

## Key insight

A review ends in calm continuity when it produces one small next instruction: **keep the readable plan for two ordinary attempts, then review again—or run exactly one supported bounded test.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a carry-forward ritual, a stability boundary, test-fidelity checks, and a six-record continuity check, while actual evidence remains unknown. This lesson converts review into an operational handoff without increasing duration, frequency, tracking burden, or output expectations. Preserve the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable approach. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger system.

## What comes next

After the two ordinary attempts named in a maintenance or evidence handoff, teach a compact two-record handoff review: confirm any current test was used, read cue then start, and either retain the instruction for another modest interval or return to the existing repeated-break boundary.
