## Lesson
0115 — Write One Decision-Ready Instruction

## What was taught

- Use this step only after both covered attempts passed the enactment audit and were classified cue-first.
- Turn mixed, working, or unclear evidence into one retain-and-gather instruction for two ordinary attempts.
- Only when the same readable `cue` or `start-side` label appears twice may the instruction include one already-specified matching bounded test.
- Make the sentence auditable by naming the existing observable cue, exact two-minute action, and two-attempt boundary.

## Key insight

A classified pair supports one calm next move, not an overhaul: **retain and gather, or run one already-specified matching test—then let two ordinary attempts supply the evidence.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can audit whether an evidence-bound instruction was followed and classify an audit-passed pair cue-first. This lesson adds only the translation from that pair to one decision-ready sentence. Preserve the observable cue, exact two-minute action, two-attempt boundary, one-variable limit, and no-penalty stance. Do not add a duration target, output target, reminder, second test, catch-up work, or character explanation.

## What comes next

Audit the exact Lesson 115 instruction against its two covered ordinary attempts. Label each `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`; if either is not `followed`, repeat the exact instruction unchanged for two ordinary attempts before classifying cue or start outcomes.
