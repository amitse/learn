# Learning Record 0012 — Reset After a Disrupted Week

## Date

2026-08-01

## Lesson

0012 — Reset After a Disrupted Week

## What was taught

- After a disrupted week, the learner writes one reset card rather than creating a catch-up plan.
- The reset card reuses the existing primary cue, two-minute action, and visible finish line.
- The learner selects at most one adjustment from the previous review, or deliberately selects no change when the pattern is unclear.
- The next practice is one ordinary attempt with the established evidence fields; it does not add reminders, duration, or compensation.

## Key insight

A disruption changes neither the mission nor the size of the next move: **reuse the smallest ritual, test one link—or no link—and make one calm attempt.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner has a complete bounded ritual and has now reviewed seven days of evidence, but consistency remains unproven. This lesson protects the ritual from all-or-nothing recovery behaviour. Keep the primary cue and one fallback intact, preserve stopping as valid, and avoid interpreting a count or missed days as a personal verdict.

## What comes next

Lesson 0013 should teach how to distinguish a genuine system adjustment from an accidental redesign, so the learner can keep experiments interpretable over the remaining days.
