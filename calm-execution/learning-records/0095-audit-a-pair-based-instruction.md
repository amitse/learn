# Learning Record 0095 — Audit a Pair-Based Instruction

## Date
2026-10-23

## Lesson
0095 — Audit a Pair-Based Instruction

## What was taught

- Compare each of the two covered attempts with the exact Lesson 94 sentence before reading cue or start outcomes.
- Label each attempt `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear` from the written instruction and record.
- When either attempt is `not followed` or `unclear`, repeat the identical instruction for two ordinary attempts without redesign, catch-up work, or outcome interpretation.

## Key insight

A pair of outcomes can inform the ritual only after its instruction was enacted: **audit the exact sentence first; repeat it unchanged until both attempts are readable as followed.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can turn a classified pair into a bounded instruction naming one move, cue, exact two-minute action, and two-attempt boundary. This lesson adds the immediate fidelity gate while preserving the no-penalty stance and one-variable boundary. Do not introduce duration, output targets, a second test, or a character explanation.

## What comes next

When both covered attempts are `followed`, classify the pair cue-first: read `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`; label each `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear` before deciding whether to retain and gather or use one already-specified matching test.
