# Learning Record 0061 — Write a Classified-Pair Instruction

## Date
2026-09-19

## Lesson
0061 — Write a Classified-Pair Instruction

## What was taught

- The learner converts the Lesson 60 pair classification into one exact instruction for the next two ordinary attempts.
- A mixed, workable, or unclear pair produces a retain-and-gather instruction; it does not justify a new change.
- Only two matching readable `cue` labels permit one already-specified cue test; only two matching readable `start-side` labels permit one already-specified setup or opening-action test.
- The instruction names the existing observable cue and exact two-minute action so its enactment can be audited before outcomes are interpreted.

## Key insight

A classified pair becomes operational only when it yields one checkable sentence: **retain the small plan or use one supported test, then enact that exact sentence twice.**

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can classify a verified pair but needs a bounded bridge from classification to the next ordinary attempts. This writing drill preserves the two-minute baseline, chosen frequency, no-penalty stance, and one-variable boundary. It neither adds tracking burden nor treats a small record set as causal proof. Do not add catch-up work, multiple changes, or a larger planning system.

## What comes next

After the instruction covers two ordinary attempts, teach an enactment audit: compare the exact Lesson 61 sentence with both attempts, label each `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear`, and repeat the instruction unchanged whenever enactment is not yet readable.
