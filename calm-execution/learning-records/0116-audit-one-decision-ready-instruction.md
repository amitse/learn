## Lesson
0116 — Audit One Decision-Ready Instruction

## What was taught

- Before interpreting the two attempts covered by the Lesson 115 instruction, compare each record with that exact sentence.
- Label each attempt `followed`, `not followed`, or `unclear` based on whether the stated cue, exact two-minute action, and any active test were used.
- If either label is `not followed` or `unclear`, repeat the instruction unchanged for two ordinary attempts; do not classify cue or start outcomes yet.
- Only an audit-passed pair (`followed`, `followed`) proceeds to cue-first outcome classification.

## Key insight

**Enactment is part of the evidence.** An outcome cannot evaluate a written instruction that was not clearly used.

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can write one decision-ready instruction from an audit-passed, classified pair. This lesson adds one narrow verification gate: distinguish an enacted instruction from an unclear or changed attempt. Preserve the observable cue, exact two-minute action, two-attempt boundary, one-variable limit, and no-penalty stance. Do not add a duration target, output target, reminder, catch-up work, second test, or character explanation.

## What comes next
