## Lesson
0119 — Classify One Audit-Passed Pair

## What was taught

- After both covered attempts pass the enactment audit, read each record in a fixed order: `cue occurred?`, then `two-minute action started?`.
- Label each attempt `cue`, `start-side`, `none`, or `unclear`.
- Retain and gather records for working, mixed, or incomplete evidence.
- Only a repeated readable `cue` or `start-side` break makes one already-specified matching bounded test eligible; do not add multiple changes, urgency, or a consequence.

## Key insight

**A pair becomes decision-ready only when it is readable twice in the same way.** A repeated label narrows the next experiment; it does not turn a miss into a character verdict.

## Zone of proximal development notes

The learner can audit whether a decision-ready instruction was followed across two ordinary attempts. This lesson adds only cue-first classification of an audit-passed pair. Preserve the observable cue, exact two-minute action, two-attempt boundary, one-variable limit, and no-penalty stance. Do not add a duration target, output target, reminder, catch-up work, second test, or character explanation.
