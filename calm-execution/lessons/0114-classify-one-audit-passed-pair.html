Calm Execution · Lesson 114 · 8 minutes

Classify one audit-passed pair

Once both attempts followed the instruction, read what happened—cue first, start second.

The one skill

Use this only after Lesson 113’s audit says followed, followed. Give each attempt one label:

cue — the stated cue did not occur or was not noticed.
start-side — the cue occurred, but the exact two-minute action did not start.
none — the cue occurred and the action started.
unclear — the record cannot tell.

This is a practical reading rule, not a judgment. It separates a missing cue from a missed opening action so you do not redesign everything at once. Repeating a behaviour in a stable context can support cue-based routines; see Gardner, Lally & Wardle (2012) for background.

Applied exercise — read your pair

Spend 5–10 minutes with the two records that passed your enactment audit. Choose one label for each. Do not repair, add a reminder, or create a new test during this exercise.

Instant check

Both attempts followed the instruction. Their labels are start-side, start-side. What does this pair support?

Read the system, not your character. Next: turn this classified pair into one evidence-bound next instruction. Ask your teacher if a record feels ambiguous.
