Calm Execution · Lesson 109 · 10 minutes

Write one next instruction from a classified pair

Turn two neutral labels into one small plan you can actually check.

The one skill

Use this only after both attempts passed the enactment audit and you classified them cue-first. Your next instruction has four parts:

one move + the same cue + the exact two-minute action + two ordinary attempts

If the pair is mixed, working, or unclear, the move is retain and gather. If both labels are the same readable cue or start-side break, use one already-specified matching bounded test. Nothing else changes.

This is a practical planning rule, not a verdict about motivation. For background on stable-context habit practice, see Gardner, Lally & Wardle (2012).

Applied exercise — make your next card

Spend 5–10 minutes with the two labels from your audit-passed pair. Fill this in, then copy the generated sentence into your usual record.

Instant check

Your labels are none / start-side. What is the next move?

Keep this instruction for two ordinary attempts before auditing enactment. No urgency, guilt, catch-up work, longer duration, or second test is required. Ask your teacher if the pair or matching test is hard to name.
