Calm Execution · Lesson 80 · 10 minutes

Audit a classified-pair instruction

Before reading cue or start results, check whether the two ordinary attempts used the exact instruction from Lesson 79.

Audit the sentence, not your intention or result. For each attempt: followed, not followed, or unclear.

The one skill

  1. Place your exact Lesson 79 instruction beside the two records it covers.
  2. For each record, compare its observable cue, exact two-minute action, and stated next move with that sentence.
  3. Mark followed only when the record clearly matches all three. Mark not followed for a difference and unclear when the record cannot tell you.
  4. If either label is not followed or unclear, repeat the same instruction for two ordinary attempts. Do not interpret outcomes or add a change yet.

This is a practical course experiment: it keeps using a plan separate from judging its outcome. It is not a diagnosis or a verdict about you.

Practice: audit the two attempts

Allow 5–10 minutes. Use the two attempts covered by your Lesson 79 instruction.

Close the loop

Record both labels beside the instruction. If both are followed, preserve the records for a cue-first outcome reading next. Otherwise, repeat the exact same instruction for two ordinary attempts—no catch-up, redesign, guilt, or punishment. Ask your teacher agent if a record is hard to label.

Background reading: Gardner, Lally & Wardle, “Making health habitual” (2012). The audit rule on this page is a practical course exercise.
