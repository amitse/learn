Calm Execution · Lesson 113 · 8 minutes

Audit one evidence-bound instruction

Before reading results, check whether you actually used the sentence you wrote.

The one skill

Take the exact instruction from Lesson 112 and compare it with each of its two covered attempts. For each attempt, choose one label:

followed — the stated cue, exact two-minute action, and any active test were used.
not followed — you know a named part was not used.
unclear — the record cannot tell.

If either attempt is not followed or unclear, repeat the exact instruction for two ordinary attempts. Do not interpret cue or start outcomes yet. This is a practical measurement rule: it keeps a changed plan from being confused with its results. Stable contexts can support cue-based routines; see Gardner, Lally & Wardle (2012) for background.

Applied exercise — audit your pair

Spend 5–10 minutes with your written instruction and its two records. No repair, catch-up, or new test today.

Instant check

Your labels are followed, unclear. What comes next?

Evidence first, interpretation second. A label is not a verdict about you. Next: if both attempts are followed, classify them cue-first; otherwise repeat the same instruction. Ask your teacher if a label feels ambiguous.
