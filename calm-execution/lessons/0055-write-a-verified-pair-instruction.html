Calm Execution · Lesson 55

Write a verified-pair instruction

Turn the pair you just classified into one sentence you can actually follow—and audit—over the next two ordinary attempts.

8–12 minutes

One skill

Write a complete next instruction after a verified pair: one supported move, your stable cue, and your exact two-minute action.

This is a practical experiment, not a verdict. Do not add duration, catch-up work, or a second change. The next two attempts are ordinary attempts.

Why preserve a stable context? Habit research distinguishes cue-triggered repetition from mere frequency; use this lesson’s stable cue as a small, testable practice context. Gardner, Lally & Wardle (2012).

Real-world exercise

  1. Take the two attempts that passed your instruction audit.
  2. Use your Lesson 54 labels. Choose retain/gather for mixed, working, or unclear labels. Choose one matching test only if both readable labels match and you already specified that test.
  3. Write the sentence below on your start card or beside your first object. Use it for the next two ordinary attempts.

Finish calmly

If your instruction is complete, copy it now. At each cue, do only the named two-minute action. Record the attempt. After two attempts, audit whether this exact sentence was followed before reading cue/start outcomes.
