Calm Execution · Lesson 58

Write a verified-pair next instruction

Turn the pair you just classified into one small sentence that tomorrow can actually follow—and the next review can actually check.

8–12 minutes

One skill

Write a next instruction with one supported move plus the stable cue and exact two-minute action.

This is a practical planning exercise, not a claim that two records establish a cause. If the pair was mixed, workable, or unclear, the supported move is simply: retain the plan and gather two readable ordinary attempts. Use one already-specified matching test only when the same readable break appeared twice.

Context: habits can be understood as cue-triggered responses in stable contexts; this lesson uses that idea only to keep the cue explicit. Gardner, Lally & Wardle (2012).

Real-world exercise

  1. Take the two records you classified in Lesson 57. Confirm both covered attempts were labelled followed before using their outcome labels.
  2. Write your current cue and exact two-minute action below. Choose the supported next move: retain/gather, or one already-specified matching test.
  3. Copy the generated instruction into your start record. Use it for the next two ordinary attempts—no catch-up work, extra duration, or added changes.

Instruction builder

Finish calmly

One sentence is enough. Its job is not to guarantee a result; it makes the next two attempts readable. Ask your teacher: “Here are my pair labels and instruction. Does this keep the cue and two-minute action stable?”
