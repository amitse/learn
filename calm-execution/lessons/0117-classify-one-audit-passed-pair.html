Calm Execution · Lesson 117 · 8 minutes

Classify one audit-passed pair

When both attempts followed the written instruction, read each record in the same order: cue first, then the two-minute start.

The one skill

Use this only after Lesson 116 produced followed, followed. For each record, ask: did the observable cue occur? If yes, did the exact two-minute action start?

cue = cue did not occur.
start-side = cue occurred; action did not start.
none = cue occurred; action started.
unclear = the record cannot support one of those readings.

This is a practical reading rule, not a verdict about you. It preserves the course’s cue-based design and a stable comparison. For background on stable-context repetition, see Gardner, Lally & Wardle (2012).

Applied exercise — classify your pair

Spend 5–10 minutes with the two records that passed your enactment audit. Do not repair, explain, or change anything while classifying.

Instant check

A record says: “Cue happened; I did not open the document.” Which label fits?

Finish with labels, not a fix. In the next lesson, turn the classified pair into one small, evidence-bound next instruction. Ask your teacher if a record cannot show whether the cue or action occurred.
