Calm Execution · Lesson 57

Classify a verified pair

Once both attempts passed the instruction audit, locate the earliest readable break: cue first, then the two-minute start.

7–10 minutes

One skill

Give each audited attempt one label: cue, start-side, none, or unclear.

This is a practical reading exercise, not a diagnosis. Read what the record says: a missing cue is cue; a cue that occurred but no two-minute action began is start-side; both yes is none; a missing field is unclear.

Real-world exercise

  1. Take the two attempts that were both labelled followed in Lesson 56. If either was not followed or unclear, stop: repeat that instruction for two ordinary attempts instead.
  2. For attempt 1, read only cue occurred? and then two-minute action started?. Choose its label below. Repeat for attempt 2.
  3. Use the feedback to write one next move. Keep your cue, exact two-minute action, and chosen frequency unchanged unless the same readable break appears twice and you already have one matching test specified.

Pair classifier

Finish calmly

Write the resulting instruction where tomorrow’s cue can meet it. A workable, mixed, or unclear pair means retain and gather two readable ordinary attempts. This preserves the mission: reliable starts without urgency, guilt, or punishment.

Ask your teacher: “Here are my two audited records. Which earliest-break labels are actually supported?”
