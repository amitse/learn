Calm Execution · Lesson 91

Write a classified next instruction

Turn your pair’s labels into one sentence you can actually audit after two ordinary attempts.

One sentence = one move + the same observable cue + the exact two-minute action + two attempts.

Mixed, working, or unclear labels mean retain and gather. The same readable cue or start-side label twice may use one already-specified matching test. Everything else stays stable.

This is a practical planning exercise, not a claim that two records establish a cause. Its purpose is to make tomorrow’s small start observable without adding pressure.

Your 10-minute real-world exercise

Use the pair you classified in Lesson 90. Choose the supported move. Then write one instruction and use it for the next two ordinary attempts.

Do not invent a test today. “After coffee” is a cue; “open the project and write the first heading for two minutes” is an action.

Boundary: stopping after the two-minute action remains valid. No catch-up work, guilt, or extra change is required.

Next: audit whether this exact sentence was followed in both attempts. Ask your teacher if the labels, matching-test boundary, or wording is unclear.
