Calm Execution · Lesson 53

Audit the next instruction

Before reading what happened, check whether the sentence you wrote was actually followed—twice.

One skill: make enactment readable

Take the exact next-instruction sentence from Lesson 52 and place it beside its two covered ordinary attempts. For each attempt, compare what the sentence said with what the record shows.

Label each attempt: followed, not followed, or unclear.
If either label is not followed or unclear, repeat the same instruction for two ordinary attempts. Do not interpret cue or start outcomes yet.

This is a practical reading rule: it prevents a missing or different instruction from being mistaken for an answer about the plan. Two followed labels create a pair that is ready for the existing cue-then-start classification.

8-minute applied exercise

  1. Copy your exact Lesson 52 instruction at the top of two recent records.
  2. For attempt 1, choose one label below. Do the same for attempt 2. Use unclear when the record cannot answer; do not fill gaps from memory.
  3. Use the feedback to write the one next move: repeat unchanged, or classify the verified pair next.

Two-attempt audit

Keep the cue, exact two-minute action, frequency, and any active one-link test unchanged. This exercise asks for readable evidence—not catch-up work, guilt, or a longer session. Ask your teacher if the sentence or a record is hard to read.
