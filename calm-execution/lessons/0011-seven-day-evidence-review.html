Calm Execution · Lesson 11

Run a seven-day evidence review

Count starts, then inspect one link in the system—without turning either into a verdict.

One skill

Once a week, count intentional starts from the last seven records and choose one system link to inspect next.

An intentional start means you began the pre-chosen two-minute action—at the primary cue or the one fallback cue. A stop after the finish line still counts. This count is simply progress toward the 20-in-30-day mission, not a streak to defend or a reason to add catch-up work.

Repeated behaviour in a stable context can become more cue-driven over time; this review is a practical way to see whether your small, stable ritual is getting used. Gardner, Lally & Wardle (2012).

Keep the review narrow

Do not diagnose your character from seven days. Read the records in order: cue → setup → action. Choose the earliest link that most often broke, or choose “no clear pattern.” One review produces one small experiment, not a redesign.

Tomorrow’s rule

Use the review to alter only one link. If you selected “no clear pattern,” change nothing yet. A quiet, usable ritual is more valuable than a complicated system that creates pressure.
