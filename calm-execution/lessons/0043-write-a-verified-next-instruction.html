Calm Execution · Lesson 43

Write a verified next instruction

Turn the pair you just read into one sentence that can be checked after two ordinary attempts.

The one skill

Lesson 42 gave a verified pair one of three readings: unclear, the same break twice, or anything else. Your skill now is to write the matching next instruction without redesigning the ritual.

Next move + stable cue + stable two-minute action + two ordinary attempts.

This is a practical planning exercise, not a claim that one pair explains procrastination. The cue remains named because research on habit distinguishes cue-linked automaticity from simply repeating a behaviour; see Gardner, Lally & Wardle, “Making health habitual”.

Your 8-minute exercise

  1. Take the two attempts that passed the audit and their Lesson 42 labels.
  2. Select their result below. Name your current cue and exact two-minute action.
  3. Generate one instruction and copy it beside the records.
  4. Use that one instruction for the next two ordinary attempts. Record cue occurred? and action started? No catch-up work.

Instruction builder

Use the result already established by your verified-pair reading.

Good enough

An unclear pair does not call for a better explanation. Write the gather instruction, make two ordinary attempts, and let the next records be readable. Ask your teacher if you want help making the cue or action field concrete.

The aim is one calm instruction you can later audit—not a verdict about your effort.
