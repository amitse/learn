Calm Execution · Lesson 95 · 10 minutes

Audit a pair-based instruction

Before reading cue or start outcomes, check whether each of the two attempts actually followed your written instruction.

The one skill

Compare each covered attempt with the exact sentence you wrote in Lesson 94. Give each attempt one label:

  1. Followed — its move, cue, exact two-minute action, and two-attempt boundary match the sentence.
  2. Not followed — a known part did not match.
  3. Unclear — the record cannot show whether it matched.

If either attempt is not followed or unclear, repeat the same instruction for two ordinary attempts. Do not interpret cue or start outcomes yet.

A practical experiment

Try this as a small reading rule, not a judgment: first establish whether the plan was used; only then let outcomes inform the next decision. A mismatch is system data—there is nothing to make up or punish.

Applied exercise — audit your pair

Spend 5–10 minutes with your Lesson 94 sentence and its two covered attempts. Read the sentence first. Then make one label per attempt using only what your records show.

Instant check

Attempt A followed the exact sentence. Attempt B used the same cue and action, but added a new test that was not in the sentence. What is the right next move?

Ask your teacher if the written sentence or either record is hard to read. The next useful action is a calm, readable attempt—not urgency or catch-up work.
